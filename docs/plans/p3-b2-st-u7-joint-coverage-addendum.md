## 2. Problem

**Notation.** For one path node with opacity `o`, at a pixel center `p`:

- `f = (f.r, f.g, f.b, f.a)` and `s` are the straight fill and stroke colors;
- `c_F(p)` and `c_S(p)` are the O02 coverages of the fill region and of the stroke region, each from its own boundary ([O02 :100-104](p3-o02-a5-coverage-contract.md#coverage-rule-q2-q3));
- `F' = c_F·(f.rgb·f.a, f.a)` and `S' = c_S·(s.rgb·s.a, s.a)` are coverage-adjusted premultiplied paints;
- `D` is the destination before the path.

**The target**, fixed by P1 D2 (`docs/plans/p1-instanced-primitives.md:154`) and restated by the B2 contract (`docs/plans/p3-b2-stroke-contract.md:138`):

```text
L = S' + F'·(1 − S'.a)
P = o·L
result = P + D·(1 − P.a)
```

**What ST-U6 does.** Two source-over draws, fill then stroke, give `S + F·(1 − S.a) + D·(1 − F.a)·(1 − S.a) = L + D·(1 − L.a)`, which is exact at `o = 1` ([B2 contract :564](p3-b2-stroke-contract.md#96-composition-and-the-opacity-staging-b2-q6)). With `0 < o < 1`, drawing `o·F'` then `o·S'` gives `o·S' + o·F'·(1 − o·S'.a) + D·(…)`. In the overlap, the fill term carries `(1 − o·S'.a)` instead of `(1 − S'.a)`: the stroke hides the fill only partly, so opacity is in effect applied twice. On the approved example the overlap becomes `(0.25, 0, 0.5, 0.75)` instead of `(0, 0, 0.5, 0.5)` (`docs/plans/p3-visible-semantics-proposal.md:31`). That is why ST-U6 reports `composition-unsupported` and draws neither paint (`packages/renderer-core/src/path-mesh-pipeline.ts:439-457`).

**Where the difficulty is.** The coverage `c_S` varies inside one fill triangle: the stroke boundary crosses fill triangles, and the AA band of the stroke lies over fill pixels. Any exact scheme must know `c_S` (or `c_F`) at every pixel where both are nonzero, attributed separately (`docs/plans/p3-private-contract.md:185`), with opacity reaching each sample once (`docs/plans/p3-v3-renderer-mesh-pipeline-contract.md:402`).

### 3.2 Why the fill draw must carry `c_S`

Three draw orders were checked against ST7-J1 with P1's premultiplied `one / one-minus-src-alpha` blend (`docs/plans/p3-o02-a5-coverage-contract.md:201`):

- **Fill draw corrected (fill first, then stroke).** The stroke draw outputs `o·S'` unchanged. The fill draw must output `X = o·F'·(1 − S'.a)/(1 − o·S'.a)`. Proof: after both draws the result is `o·S' + (X + D·(1 − X.a))·(1 − o·S'.a)`. The color term is `o·S' + o·F'·(1 − S'.a) = P`. For the destination term, `(1 − X.a)·(1 − o·S'.a) = 1 − o·S'.a − o·F'.a·(1 − S'.a) = 1 − P.a`. For `0 < o < 1` the denominator is at least `1 − o > 0`, and `X` is a valid premultiplied color (`0 ≤ X ≤ o·F'`). This is the proposal's scheme (`docs/plans/p3-b2-stroke-proposal.md:235`).
- **Stroke draw corrected (fill outputs `o·F'`, then stroke).** The stroke draw would need `src = P − k·o·F'` with `src.a = 1 − k` and `k = (1 − P.a)/(1 − o·F'.a)`. On the approved example `k = 1` and `src = (−0.5, 0, 0.5, 0)`. A negative premultiplied output is clamped on a unorm target, so this order is not exact.
- **Stroke first, fill second.** The fill draw would need `Y = o·F'·(1 − S'.a) + o·S'·o·F'.a·(1 − S'.a)/(1 − o·S'.a)`, which is valid but also needs `c_S`.

So in every single-pass scheme the fill draw needs `c_S` at its own pixels. The first order is the simplest and keeps the stroke draw exactly as ST-U6 emits it.

**Error transfer.** Let the fill draw use `c̃_S = c_S + δ` while the stroke draw uses `c_S`. Expanding the result exactly, its fill contribution changes by `o·F'·s.a·δ·(o − 1)/(1 − o·c̃_S·s.a)`. Because `(1 − o)/(1 − o·c̃_S·s.a) ≤ 1`, its magnitude is at most `o·c_F·f.a·s.a·|δ| ≤ |δ|`, and the destination factor changes by the same amount. A mismatch between the two draws' `c_S` therefore moves the result by at most `|δ|`, at every opacity; it is not amplified as `o → 1`.

### 3.3 Option ST7-A: joint-coverage fill draw over an overlay

**What it is.** Both meshes stay as the ports return them. For a path with both paints drawable and `0 < o < 1`, the renderer derives a **joint overlay**: the fill draw's triangles (region triangles plus the exterior triangles it keeps) split along the stroke region's boundary edges, so that every resulting piece lies on one side of the stroke boundary. Each piece carries the fill role and list of its parent fill triangle and its own stroke role and stroke feature list. A joint fill draw over these pieces evaluates `c_F` and `c_S` with the O02 rule and outputs `X` (section 3.2). The stroke draw that follows is ST-U6's, unchanged. Section 4 states it in full.

## 4. Recommendation: ST7-A

ST7-A is the only option that is exact under A5, fits the V3 packet and slot model, needs no kernel or port change, and stays inside the approved composition directions: it is a "proven single-opacity mesh scheme" with separately attributed paints (`docs/plans/p3-private-contract.md:183-185`), not independent full-alpha draws. What it adds beyond the approved text is TypeScript geometry with Steiner points, which ST7-Q2 puts to the user.

### 4.1 Drawable condition and order

- **Joint condition.** A path draws joint exactly when its fill and its stroke are both drawable in this `prepare` (B2 §9.2, :540-544), neither paint fails (§9.6, :568), and `0 < o < 1`. A path with only one drawable paint (for example a culled fill) stays a single-paint draw, exact at any opacity (B2 :564).
- **Order.** The joint fill draw takes the fill draw's place: same `position`, before the stroke draw, consecutive in `draws` (B2 :558). The stroke draw is ST-U6's, unchanged in bytes.
- **Unchanged cases.** At `o = 1` and at `o = 0`, and for every single-paint path, the packet is ST-U6's (ST7-J8).

### 4.2 The joint overlay

**Domain.** The fill draw's domain: the served fill variant's region triangles and its exterior triangles with nonempty lists, as V3 packs them (V3 :263, :364). Pixels outside this domain have `c_F = 0`, where `X = 0`, so nothing outside it is needed.

**Splitting edges.** The stroke region's `boundaryEdges` (port rule 5; V3 :273). They are exactly the stroke region boundary (V3 :273-275).

**Construction.** For each domain triangle `T`, in V3's packing order:

1. Find the stroke boundary edges whose closed segment meets the interior of `T`, with an exact bounding-box prefilter and exact `orient2d` tests on the Float64 positions.
2. If none, `T` is one piece.
3. Otherwise split `T` along those edges into convex polygons, with exact rational intersection points. A Steiner point is the intersection of a stroke boundary edge with a domain triangle edge, or a stroke boundary vertex inside `T`. A Steiner point on an edge shared by two domain triangles is computed once and used by both, so the split is conforming.
4. Round each Steiner point once per coordinate to the nearest Float64. Triangulate each convex piece by a fan from its lexicographically smallest vertex.

**Roles.** Each piece keeps its parent's fill role (0 region, 1 exterior). Its stroke role is the stroke-region membership of its exact (unrounded) centroid, decided by exact winding over the stroke boundary loops.

**Lists.**

- The fill list is the parent's list, unchanged: a piece lies inside its parent up to rounding, so every fill feature within `ρ_F′` of the piece is listed.
- The stroke list is every stroke feature (the stroke variant's EDGE and VERTEX records, V3 :358-360) within local distance `ρ_S′ = 2^f′` of the closed piece, decided exactly as the fringe builder decides its lists (V3 :262; V3-A11's brute-force check). It is sorted by `(kind, global id)` (O02 :139).
- If the stroke paint is drawn region-only (`fringe-work-cap`, V3 :408), the stroke list is empty and the stroke role decides `c_S ∈ {0, 1}`. If the fill paint is region-only, the domain is its region triangles and every fill list is empty. Section 4.4 shows that both stay consistent with the paint's own draw.

**Exact partition check.** Before publication, the rounded pieces must pass O02's independent partition check against the domain: every piece positively oriented in exact arithmetic on the rounded points, areas summing exactly to the domain area, and edges matching across neighbours (V3 :280; `docs/plans/p3-o02-a5-coverage-contract.md:188`). A failure is `overlay-unsupported` (section 4.6).

**Determinism.** The same fill variant, stroke variant and order give identical bytes.

### 4.3 Why the far-field clamp stays sound on each piece

O02's clamp returns the role constant when the nearest listed feature is at least 1.0 px away; it is sound when the list holds every feature within 1.5 px and the primitive does not cross the boundary (O02 :141-149).

- **Reach.** Each list holds every feature within local `ρ′ ≥ ρ = 2^f`, which maps to at least 2 px in every direction (V3 :178, :262), above 1.5 px.
- **One side.** Each unrounded piece lies on one side of the stroke boundary by construction, and inside its parent fill triangle.
- **Rounding.** Rounding a Steiner point moves each coordinate by at most `2^-53` times its magnitude, so by at most `√2·2^-53·(M + r)` local units in all, with `M` the path's largest source coordinate magnitude and `r` the half-width. The stroke guard ST-G1, `16·2^-53·(M + r) ≤ 2^(e−4)` (B2 :275), holds for every `ok` stroke mesh, and the tolerance rule gives `2^e·sigmaMax(S) ≤ 0.125` (V3 :176). The displacement is therefore at most `√2·2^-11 < 2^-10` physical px. A point on the wrong side of its role lies within that distance of the boundary, where `d* < 1` and the rule, not the role, decides. O02's 0.104 px margin (`:149`) absorbs it, together with the binary32 packing that V3 already accepts without a certificate (V3 :351).

### 4.4 Consistency with each paint's own draw

The joint fill draw's `c_S` must be the value the stroke draw computes at the same pixel, and `c_S = 0` wherever the stroke draw paints nothing.

- **Stroke with a fringe.** At a pixel the stroke draw paints, its triangle's list and the piece's list both hold every stroke feature within 1.5 px; both select the nearest by identical f32 distances in global order with strict `<` (O02 :139), using the stroke record's lanes and anchor. Where the stroke draw paints nothing (an empty-list exterior triangle or outside its frame), every stroke feature is more than `ρ` (2 px) away, so the rule gives 0 in the joint draw too.
- **Stroke region-only.** The stroke draw covers exactly the samples of its region triangles with `c = 1`. The pieces are split along the same boundary, so a sample's covering piece has the sample's stroke membership, up to the f32 positions of the two meshes (ST7-R4).
- **Fill region-only.** The fill draw covers its region triangles with `c = 1` (V3 :408); the joint draw covers the same domain with `c_F = 1`.
- **Bound.** Any residual mismatch `δ` moves the composite by at most `|δ|` (section 3.2).

### 4.6 Cache, accounting, timing and failures

- **Identity.** An overlay variant belongs to the pair (fill entry and its served `e′` and fringe variant or region-only mode, stroke entry and its served `e′` and fringe variant or region-only mode). It is unreachable when either parent variant is.
- **Accounting.** Its typed-array bytes count in V3's 64 MiB budget and its eviction order (V3 :220-230). At most two overlay variants per node; the least recently served goes first. Evicting a parent evicts the overlay.
- **Timing.** Built on demand in the first `prepare` that needs it (ST7-Q3), then cached; opacity toggles between 1 and below 1 reuse it. No port call in any case (ST7-J5).
- **Failure.** A failed partition check, or an overlay over the work cap (ST7-Q5), is the renderer-scope state `overlay-unsupported`, with `paint: null`: the path is not drawn, both cached meshes stay, the overlay variant is marked as V3 marks a failed fringe variant (V3 :213), and one notice is queued per mark (ST7-Q4). It clears when either parent variant or the opacity condition changes.
- **Whole-path failure.** Unchanged: if either paint fails, the path is not drawn (B2 :568).
- **Removal.** `composition-unsupported` is removed from the code, the `PathMeshFailureCode` union and the notice table in one change (V3 :439 pattern). `overlay-unsupported` is D8-interim and is removed with the TypeScript builder at the R2/K1 placement decision, as `fringe-work-cap` is (V3 :408).

### 4.7 Consequences

- **Visible.** Paths with both paints at `0 < o < 1` draw, with opacity once, instead of vanishing with `composition-unsupported`.
- **New interim failure.** `overlay-unsupported` can hide such a path (rounding inversions, the work cap). It is reported, never silent.
- **CPU.** A one-time exact overlay per node and served variant pair. Its cost adds to V4's builder-cost gate and to B0's E3 observations (V3 :544).
- **GPU.** Joint fragments evaluate two lists. No pass, attachment or texture is added.
- **Architecture.** A D8-interim exception for TypeScript Steiner points (ST7-Q2) and, under ST7-Q3 (a), a reading of `docs/graphics-engine-architecture.md:155` for derived overlay vertices. Container opacity stays 1 (ST7-Q7).
- **Disposable.** Under K1 the overlay has to be rebuilt per tile; the R2 contract's per-tile partition carries it.

## 8. Risks

| ID      | Risk                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| ST7-R1  | **CPU cost.** The overlay is exact BigInt work, quadratic in the worst case; fan-like fill triangulations crossed by the inner stroke boundary produce many Steiner points. It adds to V4's builder-cost gate and B0's E3 (V3 :544). No performance claim          |
| ST7-R2  | **Rounding inversions.** Float64 Steiner points can invert a tiny piece; the partition check then fails and the path is `overlay-unsupported`. Frequency is unknown until ST-U7d's fixtures and the playground report it                                           |
| ST7-R3  | **Cross-module f32.** The joint and fill shaders are separate modules; WGSL does not forbid different contraction, so `c_S` (and `c_F`) may differ by a few f32 ulps between draws, which moves the result by the same amount (section 3.2)                        |
| ST7-R4  | **Ties and region-only edges.** At a medial-axis f32 tie, or where a region-only stroke edge and an overlay split edge round to different f32 positions, a pixel or sample can differ by up to one coverage step; measure-zero, as O02 observes for `w` (O02 :113) |
| ST7-R5  | **G3 assumption.** Each draw relies on WebGPU's shared-edge rule, which is not normative (O02 :193); the joint draw inherits it                                                                                                                                    |
| ST7-R6  | **Fragment cost.** Joint fragments evaluate two lists; anisotropic transforms enlarge both (V3 :545)                                                                                                                                                               |
| ST7-R7  | **Memory.** Overlay variants copy fill vertices and lists; with two per node they add to the 64 MiB budget and can push a working set over it (counted in `overBudgetFrames`, V3 :230)                                                                             |
| ST7-R8  | **Thin strokes.** O02's rule on regions narrower than a pixel is untested (B2 :776); the joint draw inherits that                                                                                                                                                  |
| ST7-R9  | **Disposable.** The overlay, record word 15 and the joint pipeline are D8-interim; K1 and the C04 layout may replace them, and K1 needs a per-tile overlay                                                                                                         |
| ST7-R10 | **New interim failure.** `overlay-unsupported` adds a hide-with-notice outcome; it must be removed with the TypeScript builder (V3 :439)                                                                                                                           |
