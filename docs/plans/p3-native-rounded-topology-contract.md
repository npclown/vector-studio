Use these stages, matching P3.1f rather than inferring old geometric order:

3. Check every source's final actual point equals canonical p3. Any KnotMismatch has zero counters and precedes geometric or leaf-append capacity failures after preflight; declared-count WorkLimit still wins in stage1. No internal exact-knot requirement remains in this separate helper.
4. Build in contour/source/leaf order, carrying actual A from the prior actual endpoint globally within the contour; initialize once at its first source p0. Charge leaf capacity before append, including the fixed nonzero implicit closure. Compute exact source restriction and hull(Q0,Q1,Q2,Q3,A,B). No projection before all leaves are built. Complete all contour construction before the global minimum-three-leaves check, matching P3.1f, including when a later closure would exhaust capacity.
5. Enumerate i<j in append order, charge before inspection, orient consecutive and last-to-first pairs in contour order. Sole candidate d=next.Q3-previous.Q0. Do not reuse old publication's controls[0] assumption. Publish only after every area/nesting check succeeds. Zero area or boundary query is Unresolved.

All errors publish None; finite unresolved geometry remains a sufficient-condition limitation, not a product rejection rule. Success preserves topology of the actual line contour; separate P3.1d location and mesh-region checks remain mandatory for composition.
