# P3 Alt-A1 AL-OB5 proposal: direct measurement of the 1,000 × 32 workload

Status: DRAFT 2026-10-21, written for independent review and the user's approval. Full tier: it fixes a benchmark scenario and how its result is used against the unchanged budgets and decision rule. It is documentation only. No harness, run or record follows from it until an independent review returns READY and the user approves it and answers [section 7](#7-open-questions-for-the-user). The [P3 plan](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md) owns status. The [Alt-A1 production contract](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md) owns the obligation, the budgets' use and the decision rule; the [B0 contract](p3-b0-feasibility-benchmark-contract.md) owns the corpus construction and the budgets; the [benchmark README](../benchmarks/README.md) owns methodology and the record format. This proposal changes none of them.

**Identifiers.** This proposal prefixes its own IDs with **OB5-**: workload sets OB5-W2, OB5-W4 and OB5-W8, stages OB5-SP (port) and OB5-SR (renderer observation), validity items OB5-V1 to OB5-V14, units OB5-U1 to OB5-U3 and questions OB5-Q1 to OB5-Q7. Every other ID keeps its owner's meaning (AL- from the Alt-A1 contract, B0- and E1-E5 from the B0 contract, SL- from the spike contract, V3- from the V3 contract).

**Evidence label.** Precision stays **UNVERIFIED** against the 0.25 physical-pixel target until B4 has A04 evidence ([Alt-A1 contract :345](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#45-region-and-precision-obligations)). The scenario measures cost only.

## 1. Purpose

**Why it is owed.** The roadmap's P3 path stress scene is "1,000 paths with 32 cubic segments each" ([prototype plan :109](../prototype-plan.md#p3-fill-and-stroke-meshes)). `docs/requirements.md` states the platform targets around it, not the workload: current desktop Chrome and Edge, 10,000-node documents and 60 frames per second for typical editing ([requirements :122-128](../requirements.md#platform-and-performance)). Under the pre-B1 caps a 32-cubic path could not run, so AL-S1 extrapolated, as B0 did ([Alt-A1 contract :459](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#54-verifying-the-workload-itself)). Section 5.4 of that contract therefore requires B1's acceptance to include the workload "through the V2 adapter in headed Chrome and Edge against the same three budgets, under a scenario fixed before B1's run" (:459). That is open obligation AL-OB5 (:547). B1 merged without it under the user's AL-U8 decision of 2026-10-20, and AL-OB5 "is still owed as a later B1 acceptance measurement; it does not gate the merged B1" ([contract :592](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#8-review-and-approval-record), [plan :1874](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#alt-a1-al-u7-reference-runs)). B1 now admits one 32-cubic path at λ = 8, 256 leaves, on the fast rung ([plan :1928](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#b1-kernel-caps), :1966).

**What it decides.** Whether the production fill operation `'geometry-wasm/fill-v0+r2'` at the B1 caps meets the three B0-Q1 budgets on the workload itself, measured directly with no extrapolation ([B0 contract :271](p3-b0-feasibility-benchmark-contract.md#8-decisions-for-the-user); [Alt-A1 contract :20](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#11-authority)):

| Budget | Value                                                             | Measured here as                                    |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| B_init | 1,000 ms initial tessellation of the workload (T)                 | T, one port call on the 1,000 paths (section 3.2)   |
| B_edit | 4 ms per-path edit proxy, a quarter of a 60 Hz frame (t_edit)     | t_edit, individually timed one-path port calls      |
| B_mem  | 64 MiB of mesh payload (M), 16·V + 12·T bytes per path as B0's E2 | M, the sum over the 1,000 output records of one set |

No budget, threshold or decision rule changes ([Alt-A1 contract :70](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#16-scope-and-non-goals)). The verdict uses the existing three-verdict rule (section 4). What changes relative to AL-S1 and AL-S2 is only the evidence route: a measured T and t_edit replace the extrapolated T̂ = 1,000 · t̂ · k̂ and t̂_edit = t̂_p95 · k̂ ([contract :438](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#53-c-product-stage-verification-scenario-al-s1-and-the-verdict)). Check 2, the prediction factor that made AL-S1 and the product verdict INCONCLUSIVE ([plan :1852-1855](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#alt-a1-al-u7-reference-runs)), has no counterpart, because nothing is extrapolated.

**What it does not decide.** It does not revise the AL-S1 or AL-S2 records or the AL-U8 decision; committed records stay immutable ([README :19](../benchmarks/README.md#directory-layout)). It does not decide DF8 (the fringe builder), DF9 (the zoom-step rebuild), E4 draw cost, crossings, generic-coordinate cost or lower-tier hardware ([contract :461-473](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#55-what-this-contract-measures-and-what-it-defers)). It does not measure stroke (OB5-Q2).

## 2. Workload definition, fixed before any run

### 2.1 Generator

**Scenario corpus `p3-al-ob5-workload/v1`.** Three decision sets of 1,000 single-contour paths of 32 cubics each, built by the B0 generator `p3-b0-ring/v1` unchanged, applied to three new cells:

| Set    | C   | s (cubics) | λ (leaves per cubic) | Bulge h | Leaves L = 32λ | Flat commands 32λ + 2 | Expected fast mesh V, T, boundary pairs |
| ------ | --- | ---------- | -------------------- | ------- | -------------- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| OB5-W2 | 1   | 32         | 2                    | 3/64    | 64             | 66                    | 64, 62, 64                              |
| OB5-W4 | 1   | 32         | 4                    | 3/16    | 128            | 130                   | 128, 126, 128                           |
| OB5-W8 | 1   | 32         | 8                    | 3/8     | 256            | 258                   | 256, 254, 256                           |

- **Construction.** B0 section 2 steps 1-6, unchanged: edge length 8, circumradius r = e / (2 sin(π/s)), the right-hand-normal bulge, snapping to multiples of 2^-10 with ties to even, counterclockwise order for the one contour ([B0 contract :55-64](p3-b0-feasibility-benchmark-contract.md#2-corpus-and-sweep)). Bulges by density are B0's (:68-74; `tests/geometry/p3-b0/corpus.ts:58-62`). With s = 32, r ≈ 40.8 and every coordinate stays far below 2^11, so the snapped values are exact and closures are bit-exact (B0 :63).
- **Seed.** B0's: xorshift32 (13, 17, 5), state reset to `0x12345678` at the start of each cell, one draw ρ = 2π·u per path ([B0 contract :66](p3-b0-feasibility-benchmark-contract.md#2-corpus-and-sweep); `corpus.ts:16`, `:207-237`). Path j of each set therefore has the same rotation in all three sets, and the first n paths of a set are a prefix of its 1,000.
- **Code.** OB5-U1 calls `generateCellPaths` and `encodeCorpus` from `tests/geometry/p3-b0/corpus.ts` (:207, :239) with three new `CellSpec` values (series `OB5-W2`, `OB5-W4`, `OB5-W8`; role `decision`; family `ring`; entry `attempt`; `snapped: true`; `paths: 1000`). The B0 module is not edited. The framed corpus is a new byte stream with its own SHA-256, recorded in the plan when OB5-U1 merges; B0's corpus bytes (`206cf6e9…9796`) are unchanged.
- **Request form.** Each path is one `FillMeshRequest`: nonzero, `toleranceExponent` −3 (flatten tolerance 0.125, B0's :81), request ID = path index. Zoom 1, DPR 1 and identity transform, as B0 fixes (:78-82); the V3 tolerance rule gives e = floor(log2(0.125 / 1)) = −3 at that view ([contract :141](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#32-tolerance-range-of-the-fast-rung-al-fr0)).
- **Valid at the B1 caps.** Per path: 34 source verbs (≤ 72), 194 source scalars (≤ 392), 32 segments (≤ 64), one contour (≤ 4), 32λ + 2 ≤ 258 flat commands (≤ 264), 32λ ≤ 256 owners (≤ 256) and 32λ ≤ 256 contour points (≤ 260; one per leaf, `fill_export_tests.rs:1680`) ([plan :1930-1950](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#b1-kernel-caps)). OB5-W8 sits exactly at the owner cap, as B1's own 32-cubic ring test does (:1966). Per batch: about 1.6 MB of input for OB5-W8, far below the adapter's 64 MiB input cap (`packages/geometry-wasm/src/fill-adapter.ts:24`); about 9.3 MB of output including boundary pairs and records, below the 256 MiB output cap (:25).

**Expected outcome, fixed in OB5-U1 before any timing.** Every path of every set is `ok` on rung 0 with the V, T and boundary-pair counts of the table: a fast mesh has no Steiner vertex, its vertices are the polygon's and a simple contour gives n − 2 triangles ([contract :160-166](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#35-output-form-of-a-fast-mesh-and-the-always-on-check-al-fr9), :155). B1 shows this for three rotations of the OB5-W8 ring ([plan :1966](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#b1-kernel-caps)). OB5-U1's native test runs all 3,000 paths and commits the per-set status, rung and count summary as the expectation. If any path falls back or fails there, the generator is not changed; the finding goes into the OB5-U1 PR and the user sees it before OB5-U2 starts.

### 2.2 Why three densities

B0's target is the same workload at λ = 2, 4 and 8, L\* = 64, 128 and 256, and its estimates take the maximum over series ([B0 contract :175](p3-b0-feasibility-benchmark-contract.md#5-extrapolation-and-the-feasibility-decision), :189). The direct measurement keeps that: the verdict takes the maximum over the three sets (section 3.4). OB5-Q6 asks whether to keep only OB5-W8.

### 2.4 Admission rule and failing paths

- **100 % `ok` is required.** A decision set is valid only if every one of its 1,000 paths is `ok` in every call of every repetition, in both browsers and natively. The workload is 1,000 drawn paths; a failed path has no mesh and costs less, so a partial set would understate T and M.
- **`work-limit`, `unsupported-geometry` or any other path failure** fails validity item OB5-V5. The verdict is then INCONCLUSIVE (section 4). The failing paths are recorded with set, index, status and native failure reason. They are never dropped, replaced or re-drawn, as B0 never drops cells silently ([B0 contract :103-108](p3-b0-feasibility-benchmark-contract.md#2-corpus-and-sweep)).
- **A batch-level failure** (`batch-failed`: `resource-limit`, `internal-error`, `invalid-request`, `disposed`; `fill-adapter.ts:150-153`) fails OB5-V5 the same way.
- **A rung-1 path that is `ok`** is valid workload: it is timed as it is and counted. The rung mix must equal OB5-U1's committed expectation (OB5-V6), so an unexpected fallback is a validity failure, not a silent cost change. B1 notes that an exact-rung fallback above 64 edges runs the pre-B0R loops ([plan :1993](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#b1-kernel-caps)).

## 3. Measurement path

### 3.1 Identity, product stage and legs

- **Scenario** `p3-al-ob5-workload`, version 1, runner `p3-al-ob5-runner/1`, command `pnpm benchmark:p3-al-ob5 --profile functional|reference --environment-json <observations.json>`. The configuration and its hash follow the [README](../benchmarks/README.md#scenario-identity) (:116-118); the reference hash is pinned in a test and recorded in the plan when OB5-U2 merges.
- **What runs.** The production operation `'geometry-wasm/fill-v0+r2'`, revision 2, port version 0 (`fill-adapter.ts:13-16`), at the B1 caps. The README requires a commit or a reproducible dirty-tree record (:43, :59; [contract :449](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#53-c-product-stage-verification-scenario-al-s1-and-the-verdict)); this proposal itself requires a clean committed `main` tree for the reference run (OB5-V13).
- **Browser leg, deciding.** The release WASM, whose SHA-256 the page verifies, through `createFillMeshPort` (`fill-adapter.ts:729`) in a Vite production page, as AL-S2's page does ([plan, AL-U6](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#alt-a1-al-u6-al-s2-wasm-ratio); `tests/geometry-benchmark/p3-b0-wasm/main.ts:217-220`). Installed stable Chrome, then Edge; headed; one worker; a fresh page per repetition; no DevTools, tracing or video ([contract :398](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#52-b-b0-w-the-wasmnative-ratio)). The page is served cross-origin isolated and records `crossOriginIsolated` (`main.ts:283`), as AL-S2 pins it.
- **Native leg, report-only and the output oracle** (OB5-Q3). In the same runner session, before the browsers, a native harness runs the same sets through `FillEngine::process` with the **production caps**. It cannot reuse AL-S1's ratio mode: M7 pins `p3_fill_ladder_bench.rs` `Harness::new` to the pre-B1 cap mode ([plan :2023](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#b1-review-follow-ups); `p3_fill_ladder_bench.rs:171`), under which a 32-cubic path fails `SourceLimit` (:1956). OB5-U2 adds a new `cfg(test)` harness file and leaves AL-S1's harness and its pin unchanged. The native leg's checksums are the oracle for the browser output (section 5). Its times are reported, and so is the ratio browser over native per set, for both the OB5-SP-init medians and the OB5-SP-edit p95, labelled as AL-S2 labels k̂; neither enters the verdict.

### 3.2 Stages and their boundaries

The port is called the way V3 calls it: one `buildFillMeshes` call carries every missing request of a prepare (`PATH_MESH_LIMITS.requestsPerCall` 2^31, `packages/renderer-core/src/path-mesh-pipeline.ts:143-144`; V1 rule 3, "one call per frame", [V3 contract :469](p3-v3-renderer-mesh-pipeline-contract.md#l-acceptance-criteria-and-validation)). An edit re-requests one path.

| Stage                         | Window start                                                                                                                                                 | Window end                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Decides     |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| OB5-SP-init (T)               | Before one `buildFillMeshes` call on the set's 1,000 requests                                                                                                | After the checksum fold of that call's last result                                                                                                                                                                                                       | B_init      |
| OB5-SP-edit (t_edit)          | Before one `buildFillMeshes` call on one request                                                                                                             | After the fold of its result                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | B_edit      |
| OB5-SP-mem (M)                | Not timed                                                                                                                                                    | 16·V + 12·T bytes summed over the 1,000 results of a set, from the output records (E2, [contract :421](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#53-c-product-stage-verification-scenario-al-s1-and-the-verdict)); E2 plus 8 bytes per boundary pair is reported | B_mem       |
| OB5-SP-cold                   | Before the first `buildFillMeshes` call of a fresh adapter, whose instance is created outside timing; the window includes its reserve and arena growth       | After the fold                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | No (OB5-Q5) |
| OB5-SR (renderer observation) | Before `PathMeshPipeline.prepare` on 1,000 path nodes with a cold cache, on the already provisioned adapter of the SP stages (no arena growth in the window) | After it returns                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | No (OB5-Q1) |

- **Inside every SP window:** the adapter's validation, packing and copies, the kernel call (both rungs, AL-FR9, output writing), the adapter's header and record checks, and the fold. The fold reads every result's status, counts, position bits, indices and boundary pairs, as AL-S2's fold does ([plan, AL-U6](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#alt-a1-al-u6-al-s2-wasm-ratio)). So the window prices the whole port, which is what the product pays ([contract :401](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#52-b-b0-w-the-wasmnative-ratio)).
- **Outside every window:** fetching and hashing the WASM and the corpus, compiling, decoding the corpus into requests (built once), arena provisioning (an untimed preparation call per set), integrity reads, and everything after the fold.
- **The edit proxy is a lower bound,** as B0's E3 is ([contract :422](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#53-c-product-stage-verification-scenario-al-s1-and-the-verdict)): it excludes scene application, the V3 cache, the fringe builder, packing, upload and draw.
- **OB5-SR** is an observation of the CPU renderer pipeline with the real port and the production `PATH_MESH_LIMITS`, report-only under every option of OB5-Q1. It includes response validation, cache insertion and the fringe builder or region-only serving. OB5-W8's meshes have 256 boundary vertices, above the 128 cap, so under the shipped limits they are drawn region-only without a builder call ([plan :1886-1891](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#v4-playground-path-page); `path-mesh-pipeline.ts:381-387`); OB5-W4's 128 are at the cap, where the builder runs (the V4 cap tests, [plan :1903](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#v4-playground-path-page)). No GPU work is measured (E4 stays deferred, [contract :470](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#55-what-this-contract-measures-and-what-it-defers)).

### 3.4 Statistics

- **Per repetition, set and browser:** for OB5-SP-init the nearest-rank median, p95 and min/max of the 20 timed samples; for OB5-SP-edit the nearest-rank median, p95 and p99 of the 1,000 samples (the README's percentile rule, `ceil(p · n) − 1` on ascending samples, :108).
- **T** = the maximum over browsers, repetitions and sets of the OB5-SP-init median. B0's T̂ is median-based ([B0 contract :177-187](p3-b0-feasibility-benchmark-contract.md#5-extrapolation-and-the-feasibility-decision)); the maximum follows B0's maximum over repetitions and series (:185) and the README's "maximum per-run p95, not a pooled p95" for ceilings (:109). The maximum OB5-SP-init p95 is reported beside it (OB5-Q4). This differs from AL-S2, whose k̂ took the nearest-rank median of r over the five repetitions per browser and then the maximum over browsers ([AL-S2 record :60](../benchmarks/results/2026-10-20_p3_p3-b0-wasm-v1_chrome-edge_desktop-dkvusav_20261020T050227Z-f86bda54.md)). The maximum over browser repetitions is more conservative, so T is not like-for-like with the T̂ it is reported beside; OB5-Q4 (c) offers AL-S2's treatment.
- **t_edit** = the maximum over browsers, repetitions and sets of the OB5-SP-edit p95, as B0's edit proxy is p95-based (:183).
- **M** = the maximum over sets of the summed E2 bytes, which must be equal in every call (OB5-V3). If the expectation of section 2.1 holds it is 1,768,000, 3,560,000 and 7,144,000 bytes for OB5-W2, W4 and W8 (1.69, 3.40 and 6.81 MiB); these are arithmetic from the expected counts, not results.
- **Reported, not decided:** native medians and p95; the browser-over-native ratio per set; OB5-SP-cold; OB5-SR with its region-only and fringe counts; T and t_edit beside AL-U7's estimates T̂ 1,031.1 ms and t̂_edit 4.367 ms, and beside t̂_p95 · k̂₁ = 4.467 ms, the closer analogue of a directly measured one-path browser edit ([plan :1854](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#alt-a1-al-u7-reference-runs)), as context; `linearMemoryBytes()` per browser after the run against BC19's 256 MB ceiling ([contract :411](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#52-b-b0-w-the-wasmnative-ratio)).

## 4. Decision use

The rule is the Alt-A1 product verdict's, unchanged ([contract :441-447](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#53-c-product-stage-verification-scenario-al-s1-and-the-verdict)), with the measured values in place of the estimates:

| Verdict      | Condition                                                                                    | Consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| FEASIBLE     | Every validity item OB5-V1 to OB5-V14 passes, and T ≤ 1,000 ms, t_edit ≤ 4 ms and M ≤ 64 MiB | The verdict goes to the user. With an accepted evidence review it closes AL-OB5 as B1's acceptance measurement (:459, :547)                                                                                                              |
| INFEASIBLE   | Every validity item passes, and any of T, t_edit or M exceeds its budget                     | It goes to the user "before any further cap work" (:459). The record states which budgets fail, by how much, the per-set values and the stage split; it chooses no remedy (:444)                                                         |
| INCONCLUSIVE | Any validity item fails, other than an output-identity item                                  | As B0-Q3 fixes: like INFEASIBLE, it goes to the user ([B0 contract :273](p3-b0-feasibility-benchmark-contract.md#8-decisions-for-the-user)). The verdict stays INCONCLUSIVE                                                              |
| (no verdict) | An output-identity item fails: OB5-V1, OB5-V2, OB5-V3, OB5-V7 or OB5-V8                      | INVALID, as a run "invalidated by an oracle gate, a checksum mismatch or a nonzero `MESH_CHECK` count has no verdict" (:447). ## 8. Risks

- **Duration (planning figure, not a measurement).** Per browser page: 3 sets × 24 init calls near T (about 70 s if T is near 1 s), 3,000 edits (a few seconds) and OB5-SR, which under OB5-Q1 (a) includes about 47 s of builder time for OB5-W4 by V4's functional figure. That is about 2 to 3 minutes per page, so 10 pages take about 25 minutes; the native leg and the oracle run add tens of minutes. About an hour in all.

- **CPU contention.** Parallel agent work, builds or third-party tools on the shared machine raise sample levels. The AL-U7 runs showed unexplained level changes in check 2 beside a third-party preview tool and many interactive applications ([plan :1855](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#alt-a1-al-u7-reference-runs), :1878). The user asked for no separate CPU-load sampling, so the run relies on the operator's quiet-machine statement and the process listings (section 3.5). The maximum statistics of section 3.4 make one disturbed repetition decide, so contention biases the verdict toward INFEASIBLE, not FEASIBLE. Every repetition is retained, never replaced ([README :73](../benchmarks/README.md#run-protocol)). If the record shows one disturbed repetition, the remedy is the user's decision, not a re-run chosen afterwards.
- **Near-budget result.** AL-U7's estimates with k̂ were 1.03 × B_init and 1.09 × B_edit ([plan :1854](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#alt-a1-al-u7-reference-runs)). A direct result near the budgets is likely, and its side of the line may depend on run conditions.
- **The edit proxy is a lower bound** (section 3.2). A FEASIBLE t_edit does not show that an edit fits a frame end to end.
- **The fringe builder may dominate the renderer stage.** V4's reviewer timed the builder at about 47 ms for a convex 128-gon ([plan :1886](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#v4-playground-path-page)); by that functional figure, OB5-W4's 1,000 paths at the cap would spend tens of seconds in the builder (an estimate, not a claim). This is why OB5-SR never decides, and it lengthens the run under OB5-Q1 (a).
- **Exact-rung fallbacks are slow above 64 edges** ([plan :1993](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#b1-kernel-caps)). OB5-V6 turns an unexpected fallback into INCONCLUSIVE rather than a hidden cost change.
- **Snapped rings favour the fast rung.** Generic coordinates, multiple contours and crossings are outside the scope; OB5-Q7 (a) only observes generic-coordinate admission.
- **Kernel drift between units.** Stroke work (B2) shares the WASM. The reference run measures `main` as it is at OB5-U3, and OB5-V4 ties the native and browser legs to the same kernel sources.
- **Memory.** One B1 reserve is 6.8 MB of linear memory ([plan :2025](p3-fill-stroke-meshes.md#b1-review-follow-ups)); a 1,000-path call adds about 11 MB of input and output arena. It is observed against the 256 MB ceiling, not decided.
- **Clock.** Without cross-origin isolation the browser quantum may exceed 0.04 ms; OB5-V10 then makes the run INCONCLUSIVE rather than silently coarse.
- **One machine.** The verdict covers the reference machine only; lower-tier hardware stays open ([contract :455](p3-alt-a1-fill-ladder-contract.md#53-c-product-stage-verification-scenario-al-s1-and-the-verdict)).
