## Purpose and proof

A small positional error does not preserve connectivity. This oracle proves that a restricted collection of simple cubic contours and their actual returned polylines are connected by a continuous family of embeddings. It preserves component orientation and nesting, hence both nonzero and evenodd winding structure. It does not assert exact equality of a curved region with its polygonal approximation. Pair it with P3.1d for positional error, then separately validate a produced mesh against its line contours.

For each source cubic leaf [a,b], derive its exact restricted Bernstein controls Q0..Q3 using rational de Casteljau restriction of the original represented controls. The caller never supplies Q or a hull. Require actual returned leaf endpoints to equal Q0/Q3 exactly; a rounded but guard-admissible knot remains outside this first proof slice. Let v=Q3-Q0, which must be nonzero. Require every derivative-control projection (Q1-Q0) dot v, (Q2-Q1) dot v and (Q3-Q2) dot v to be nonnegative and at least one positive. All quadratic Bernstein basis functions are positive for interior parameters, so the projection derivative is strictly positive there. End derivatives may vanish. The chord's projection derivative v dot v is positive.

Every simultaneous homotopy H(s,u)=(1-s)*B_leaf(u)+s*L_leaf(u) is therefore leaf-injective and stays in the convex hull of Q0..Q3. All nonadjacent hulls, including hulls from different contours, must be disjoint as closed sets. Consecutive hulls in one cyclic contour must intersect in exactly their shared canonical endpoint, including the last/first pair. Those exact knots remain fixed throughout H. Thus every intermediate contour is embedded, distinct contours remain disjoint, and orientation/nesting cannot change. This is the certificate; finite probes alone cannot establish it.

Implicit fill closure is a fixed straight segment from the final endpoint to the first start, omitted only when those points are exactly equal. Its hull is that segment, and it participates in the same cyclic adjacency and separation checks. Do not discard zero-chord source leaves or epsilon-merge nodes. A zero-chord source leaf, failed projection test or inseparable hulls returns UNRESOLVED, including valid geometry for which this sufficient condition cannot prove preservation. Stationary tangents with monotone projection are allowed; general cusps, crossings, touching components, rounded knots and near-contact hull overlap are not silently accepted.

After separation, the polygon consists of each leaf's start knot in cyclic order (no duplicate terminal knot). Its exact shoelace sign gives orientation; zero area is UNRESOLVED. For each contour i, use `classifyLineFill([polygonJ], firstKnotI, 'evenodd')` from the existing exact oracle, and multiply an inside result by orientation[j]. Hull separation ensures this knot is outside every other hull band, so its winding is unchanged throughout H. These values label nesting; they do not replace separation. A boundary result is UNRESOLVED. Set the diagonal to zero. No fill-rule parameter is needed because the topology certificate preserves both rules.

## Test-only surface

Add `tests/geometry/simple-cubic-topology/oracle.ts`, `fixtures.ts` and `tests/unit/geometry-simple-cubic-topology.test.ts`. The input is `readonly (readonly CubicTopologySegment[])[]`; a segment is `{cubic, sourceVerbOrdinal, lines}` using the existing Cubic and ReferenceFlattenedLine tuple types. All source ordinals are explicit u32 values unique across the input. Canonical endpoints must match between consecutive source segments of a contour. Each contour may otherwise be open; closure follows the rule above.

Export `certifySimpleCubicTopology(contours, limits?)` and its types only from the test module. Optional limits are `{maxContours?,maxCubics?,maxLeaves?,maxPairs?}`. Defaults/absolute ceilings are 4 contours, 16 source cubics, 64 derived leaves (including closing segments), and 2016 unordered hull pairs. The first three limits allow integer 1..ceiling; maxPairs allows 0..2016. These are bounded test-carrier limits, not runtime path limits. No caller-supplied tolerance is accepted.

Result is `{ok,status,leaves,pairs,finding,certificate}`. Status is CERTIFIED, INVALID_LIMITS, INVALID_INPUT, INVALID_PROVENANCE, KNOT_MISMATCH, UNRESOLVED or WORK_LIMIT. Only CERTIFIED publishes `certificate={polygons,orientations,winding}`; every failure publishes null even after prior successful leaves/pairs. `polygons` contains ordinary finite Point tuples copied from actual knots, orientations are -1 or 1, and winding[i][j] is -1,0 or 1 as defined above. Finding is null on success and descriptive otherwise. No retained caller data, mutation or cross-call state.

For copied polygon tuples, a source segment's first leaf starts at its canonical cubic[0]; each later leaf starts at the previous returned line.end, and implicit closure starts at the final line.end. Copy each tuple rather than retaining an input array. Connectivity and exact knot equality treat positive/negative zero as the same mathematical value; copying a selected source tuple preserves its numeric values. This convention is fixed before the first fixture execution.

Exact arithmetic may reuse rational operations/IEEE decoding from the existing test support, with explicit ordinary-unit conversion as in P3.1d. Do not import native production geometry or the rounded-fill expected-carrier algorithm. Convex hulls have at most four rational vertices. An exact monotone-chain hull may remove collinear intermediate points; a straight leaf retains its two distinct endpoints. Check closed hull intersections, including vertex-on-edge, collinear overlap, containment and proper crossings; accepting only the absence of proper edge crossings is insufficient. No float determinant, approximate angle or finite perturbation decides topology.

Reuse the bounded fixture-only Bernstein generator from P3.1d by exposing its existing uniform-line function with a source-ordinal argument (default7), preserving every existing P3.1d call and output. Primary owns that small extraction; no production/package export or changed P3.1d fixture definition. Topology implementation derives restricted controls with rational de Casteljau, independently of this direct Bernstein fixture generator.

## Validation order and limits

1. Validate limit object/finite safe integer values. Any invalid option returns INVALID_LIMITS with zero counters.
2. Validate outer/contour arrays and segment/line array structure and counts. Empty outer input or empty contours are INVALID_INPUT in this nonempty-simple-contour oracle. Excess contour/cubic/input-line count returns WORK_LIMIT before inspecting that excess content. Each source segment needs at least one line. Compare the total declared source `lines.length` against maxLeaves before inspecting line contents or exact arithmetic; an excess returns WORK_LIMIT with both counters zero. Implicit closures are excluded from this sum and charged only during hull appends below. Check all finite four-control/two-coordinate cubics and finite endpoints, explicit unique u32 source ordinals and canonical segment connectivity. Malformed/sparse tuples are INVALID_INPUT; do not skip array holes.
3. Validate all provenance: expected source ordinal, safe integer depth0..20, numerator1..2^depth; each leaf interval [(n-1)/2^d,n/2^d] must abut the previous, begin at0 and terminate at1 for each source cubic. Any mismatch is INVALID_PROVENANCE with zero counters.
4. Derive exact restrictions and verify every actual knot before constructing any hull. Any mismatch returns KNOT_MISMATCH with zero counters. Counts/shape/provenance errors elsewhere therefore precede a knot mismatch. Restricted controls are bounded by the declared source count, lines and depth.
5. Build hulls in contour/source/leaf order, adding implicit closing segments after each contour's cubic leaves. Check the leaf ceiling before inspecting the next source projection or appending any hull, including closures; allow exactly the ceiling and return WORK_LIMIT before the next. `leaves` counts appended hull records. Within the ceiling, a failed source projection/zero chord returns UNRESOLVED before appending that leaf. Closure projections are inherently positive when nonzero. All failures publish null.
6. Enumerate unordered leaf pairs i<j in append order. Charge before each pair; maxPairs checked pairs are allowed, the next returns WORK_LIMIT without inspection. `pairs` counts inspected pairs, including a pair that proves UNRESOLVED. Adjacent pairs must have their exact shared endpoint as the complete hull intersection; nonadjacent/cross-contour pairs must have empty intersection. No early successful-contour publication.
7. Derive polygon orientation/nesting and publish the complete certificate only after all checks succeed. Nesting work is bounded by at most 4*3 polygon queries over 64 total edges. No unbounded refinement, retry or hidden adaptive hull subdivision occurs.

The minimum meaningful closed polygon has at least three leaves. After closure construction, fewer than three leaves in any contour is UNRESOLVED before pair checks. This avoids treating the two endpoints of a two-leaf retrace as a single allowed cyclic intersection. Input limits are oracle limits only; finite unresolved geometry is not a product failure or a relaxed P3 gate.

Each source cubic uses uniform depth2 actual knots from exact direct Bernstein evaluation. The canonical source ordinal is 7 plus its zero-based position in the complete ordered input after fixture transformations. Source reversal reverses cubic order and each cubic's controls before regenerating its exact lines. No expected value comes from the oracle.

## Rejecting controls and acceptance

Keep these controls explicit and distinct from the 47 required successes:

- Tiny closed loop cubic [(0,0),(1/16,1/16),(-1/16,1/16),(0,0)] with sole returned endpoint(0,0), identity screen. P3.1d must certify its positional error, but topology is UNRESOLVED due to zero chord. This directly rejects distance-only topology acceptance.
- Negative projection: [(0,0),(2/128,0),(-1/128,0),(1/128,0)], sole exact endpoint, plus implicit closure. Its first source leaf fails projection before any hull append.
- Closed bowtie with corners (0,0),(6,6),(0,6),(6,0), represented by four exactly linear cubics using integer one-third controls and depth0 lines. Projection passes, but nonadjacent crossing hulls return UNRESOLVED.
- Two D contours translated by (0,0) and (8,0), depth2: cross-contour endpoint contact is UNRESOLVED, not an allowed adjacency. Two identical D contours also fail, proving collinear/coincident overlap is not ignored.
- D and (1023/1024)*D, depth2: simple nested curves whose control-hull bands overlap are UNRESOLVED. This is a limitation of the sufficient certificate, not a claim of invalid source topology. The first outer restricted hull has ordered vertices (4,0),(4,1/2),(31/8,1),(117/32,47/32). The inner hull vertex (1023/256,1023/2048) lies strictly inside it: the four successive exact cross products are 1/512,33/16384,3455/65536,10877/65536, all positive.
