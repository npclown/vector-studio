## Question

What must be frozen before the first raster coverage experiment for P3 fill meshes, and which of those choices are user or architecture decisions? Stroke remains deferred. This note only inventories the obligations and proposes a first experiment.

**Key technical consequence.** A naive mesh places triangle edges on the contour. Unless both sides of that seam evaluate the same coverage function, or the seam is moved to where coverage is 0 or 1, MSAA's geometric partition mixes unequal values and 1x and 4x diverge. No sample is covered twice in a conforming mesh, but coverage can be mixed inconsistently.

Conservative rasterization is not available in WebGPU core and is out of scope. A5 is the leading candidate on these constraints. Nothing is adopted: the mechanism is an architecture decision (Q5).

## Proposed first experiment

O01 is test-only and observational, like P3.1l. It compares A1, A2 and A5 under one oracle, at 1x and 4x, with nothing adopted.

**Inputs:** the 143 C2-admitted fixture rows, plus named F07 and F11 adjacency and overlap rows, added from the private-contract fixture table where they are not among the 158.

**Oracle:** for each candidate, a test page reads back RGBA and compares it against an exact pixel-area oracle of the certified reference polygon.

**Recorded for each candidate:**

- interior and exterior error;
- edge location under P1's region-transition oracle;
- partial-coverage deviation;
- seams on internal edges;
- the 1x/4x difference.

**Choices fixed in advance.** The O01 contract fixes Q1, Q2 and Q3 prospectively, for observation only; those choices do not bind Q4 or Q5. No threshold is adopted until the user decides Q4.

**Stop conditions and gates.** Any need for offscreen isolation or stencil stops the experiment and becomes an architecture proposal. O01 needs its own frozen contract, independent review and stable-source review before GPU dispatch.
