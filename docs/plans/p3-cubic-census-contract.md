## Bounded source reasoning

For each source cubic relative to p0, every coordinate of p1,p2,p3 lies in[-100,100]. The Bernstein second differences satisfy coordinate bounds300 and400. On a dyadic cell of width2^-d, the restricted second differences are convex combinations scaled by4^-d, and chord-deviation controls satisfy q1=-(2D0+D1)/3 and q2=-(D0+2D1)/3. Thus their Euclidean norms are <=400sqrt(2)/4^d. Depth7 is a conservative sufficient bound for1/8 including the existing small numeric guard in this bounded coordinate domain; depth6 is not established by this bound. Do not assume arbitrary chord-deviation controls themselves contract by1/4 at every split.

The conservative diagnostic ceiling is128 emitted leaves/cubic,4096 per path; at most4097 normalized fill edges with implicit closure and8,390,656 unordered pairs. These are prospective work/storage warnings, not measured maxima or proposed runtime capacities. Do not allocate quadratic/cubic event/section tables for this diagnostic. Current source caps already reject33verbs/194scalars/32cubics; record this structural fact separately from geometric preparation findings.

## Preparation-only inspector

Original coordinates have magnitude<=3200 and exact relative coordinates<=100.
