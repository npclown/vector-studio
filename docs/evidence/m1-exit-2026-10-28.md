## Exit-gate mapping

The M1 exit gate of the [MVP editor plan, M1](../plans/mvp-editor-plan.md#m1-document-v2-and-containers):

1. A custom node inside a group survives migration and round trip; v1 fixtures migrate byte-for-byte to the committed v2 fixtures; every v2 node type round-trips: M1-A01.
2. Nested group, frame and mask documents project to scenes the renderer mirror accepts: M1-A09, with the depth limits of M1-A07.
3. Undo of group, ungroup and reparent is byte-identical: M1-A05.
4. The headed next-host page shows a framed, masked, rotated document in Chrome and Edge: M1-A12.

The other M1 bullets map as follows: pages and the new commands to M1-A03 and A04, the mask-group rule and refit to A06, clip depth to A07, rotated, group and frame resize to A08, and hit testing to A10. The tools are A11. The 10,000-node smoke at the M1 exit (plan section 8) is A13. In the plan's requirement coverage (section 3), M1 closes its share of four rows: pages and the migration policy (Document), ellipse, line, arrows and corner radii (Shapes), group, frame and mask group (Containers) and custom nodes in containers and through `migrate` (Extension direction). Save and load (M5), gradients and dashes (M4) and isolated opacity (M4) stay open. R-P, which the plan files at the M1 exit (section 5), is not filed by this record.

## Known limits and disclosed findings

- **Forced isolation is slow at scale.** A forced isolation of the 10,000-node page took about 5.4 min, and each gesture update took about 86 ms. Renderer-core mirror validation was 74% to 99.9% of that time, and the projector's O(n) whole-page diff also contributes. Before M9 this needs a cheaper isolation path or a fallback to a full snapshot. Follow-up candidate, no number assigned.
- Fill-mode round caps are flattened to 16 segments, within 0.25 px up to about zoom 21 at w = 5 (M1-U5 note 2). The kernel failure on κ-cubic caps is input to E14 (note 5).
- Stroke-mode round caps can lose their coverage fringe above 128 boundary vertices. This is the existing P3 O2 class (M1-U5 note 7), not specific to M1.
- The `handles` public query was specified after its code, under the standing rule of 2026-10-24 (M1-U5 note 1).
- The headed evidence is from the main PC only.
- Precision is UNVERIFIED for M1 output, as for every renderer output. P3 exited on 2026-10-27 with disclosed defects: A04 measured FAIL on the exit snapshot, with remedies in E20 ([X1 index](../plans/d9-rendering-track.md#p3-exit-evidence-index-x1)). M1 changes no renderer code.
- Rendering items R-G, R-D and R-O are reserved for M4 as E23 to E25 (contract section 9). The rendering track has no rows for them yet.
- The M1 exit does not establish MVP completion; M2 to M9 remain.
