## Scope and method

The separate playground fixture drives the existing retained service, native packet consumer and backend lifecycle. No renderer product code, public contract or external dependency is changed. Frozen V01-V07/N02 inputs are owned by the [private contract](../plans/p1-private-contract.md); the active plan retains all color, edge and precision thresholds.

Headed tests use raw premultiplied canvas texture readback, preserving full PNGs with explicit unpremultiplication only for PNG encoding. Test instrumentation selects the existing 1x fallback, transparent clear for V02/V03 and COPY_SRC surface usage. Neither submission counts nor readback completion are display-presentation measurements.

A separate test-only vertex wrapper evaluates the captured production vertex body in the native vertex stage and observes its four guarded endpoints through float attachments. Independent f64 scene/camera arithmetic compares visible clipped contour and guard intervals with affine interpolation of those endpoints. Raw endpoints and comparison samples are retained. This does not claim fragment-stage readback; unmodified shader raster evidence remains independently required.
