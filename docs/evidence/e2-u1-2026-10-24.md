# E2-U1 evidence: lossless stroke seam merge, `stroke-v0+r5`, 2026-10-24

Rendering-track item E2, unit E2-U1 of the [E2 contract](../plans/d9-e2-stroke-fast-rung-contract.md#9-units) (E2-K1, the `MAX_PIECES` reading of section 7, revision `r5`, E2-F1 to E2-F8 recorded, E2-A4, E2-D1). Full tier. This is functional evidence. It makes no performance claim (E2-G3), and precision stays **UNVERIFIED** until E3 measures it.

## What changed

- `packages/geometry-wasm/kernel/src/stroke_merge.rs` (new): E2-K1. Step 1 builds a polygon per emitted piece: a band piece (a line sweep or cubic leaf whose band quad is strictly convex and positive, exact `orient2d`) gives the boundary-inclusive hull of its quad and its kept samples `C`; any other piece gives its hull unchanged. Step 2 drops a `C` only when it is strictly outside its seam edge, on the inner side of or on the quad's other three edges, and lies in the closed quad of a band piece whose triple on the other side of the seam is bit-identical. That piece is found by binary search over the band pieces sorted by start triple and by end triple, ties in record order. Step 3 cancels opposite directed edges with bit-identical end points: within each group of edges on the same two end points, the k-th edge of one direction cancels the k-th edge of the other, both in emission order. Step 4 traces the remaining edges into contours. Each contour starts at the first untraced edge in emission order, takes the first untraced outgoing edge at each vertex, and closes at its first return to its start vertex. Storage is reserved once: 2,229,896 B natively.
- `stroke_outline.rs`: the merge runs at the end of `front_end`. Over 512 contours the path is `PieceLimit`, and over 1,024 contour vertices it is `InputLimit`. Emission no longer stops at 512 pieces. It is bounded by the 4,096 records and by `MAX_PIECE_POINTS` = 32,768 stored points; over that cap the path is `PieceLimit`. The arrangement receives the contours. The hull routine became the free function `boundary_hull`, shared with the merge, with unchanged behaviour.
- Identity: `STROKE_V0_REVISION` is 5, and the operation id is `geometry-wasm/stroke-v0+r5` in the adapter, its tests, the GPU specs and the playground comment. B2 §7.1 and §7.6 and the E11 identity row (E2-R5) gain dated notes.
- Test-only switches in `StrokeDefects`: `drop_outside` (E2-D1) and `unmerged`. `unmerged` is not a defect: it is the `r4` path (no merge, 512 emitted pieces, `InputLimit` on the raw total, 1,024 stored points) for the A/B runs. The E1 fixtures and E1-C1 sweep, the ST-F16 and ST-F15 R3 `r4` rows, and the `r4` causes of ST-F26 now run through it. Those fixtures were fixed on the `r4` arrangement input, and E1-K1 is a property of the arrangement.

**Interpretation for the review.** Section 4 step 1 says a `C` "strictly inside the quad (exact `orient2d` against the seam)" is never a vertex. The code reads "strictly inside the quad" literally: strictly inside all four edges. A `C` on the inner side of its seam but outside a side edge is therefore kept. That keeps the step-1 polygon equal to the piece's region in every case. A seam-only test would drop such a `C` and lose area. No fixture or scene stroke hit this case (every "inside" `C` counted below is inside all four edges). Step 2 is read the same way: a `C` that is also outside a side edge stays, because only the seam is shared with the neighbour. Quad corners and samples are keyed with `-0.0` normalized to `+0.0`, so cancellation does not depend on the sign of zero. The seam-triple lookup in step 2 still compares raw bits; both sides of a seam read the same stored `Triple`, and a mismatch would only keep a `C`, which is lossless.

## E2-A4, E2-D1, storage

- **E2-A4** (`lossless`, in the tests) recomputes each check independently. (i) It checks that the step-1 polygons are the emitted pieces in order. Each band polygon must be convex and positive, its vertices must be quad corners or samples `C`, and it must hold its quad. Every `C` strictly inside the quad must not be a vertex, and every dropped `C` must be strictly outside its seam alone and inside a covering quad, found by a linear search over all pieces. Each non-band polygon must equal its hull. The merged edge multiset must equal the step-1 multiset minus the opposite pairs. (ii) It compares exact winding numbers of the contours and of the hull pieces at 4,096 seeded points (256 in the differential) plus the centroid of every triangle of the `r4` mesh, skipping points on an edge. It passes on all nine fixtures and on every case of the differential below.
- **E2-D1** (`e2_d1_dropped_outside_sample_is_caught`): the fixture `M 0.1 0.3 L 10.7 3.3` (w 2, butt) has a band piece whose end `C` is strictly outside its end seam, with no neighbour. E2-A4 passes on it. With `drop_outside`, (i) fails with "a dropped C has no covering quad".
- **ST-D13** (a clockwise piece) now reverses the step-1 polygon of odd band line sweeps, because a band polygon is rebuilt from its samples; a non-band odd sweep keeps the hull reversed at emission. It is still caught by ST-F14 (b)'s area.
- **Memory** (`e2_storage_fits_the_heap_cap`, x86_64): front end 3,992,920 B, including the merge's 2,229,896 B; `RoundedFillWorkspace` 14,262,272 B; `FillScratch` 2,654,208 B; total 20,909,400 B, within the 48 MiB `STROKE_V0_HEAP_CAP`. `build` allocates nothing (`e2_merge_is_deterministic_and_allocates_nothing`, also the existing `build_allocates_nothing`).
