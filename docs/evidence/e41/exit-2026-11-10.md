## Disclosures

- **4K full-screen effects.** One full-screen effect root at 3840 × 2160 needs about 180 MB and is over the 96 MiB budget. It draws without its effects, with one `render.effect-resource-limit` notice (section 5.4). Effect tiling is backlog.
- **Shadows inside mask groups.** In a subtree that contains a mask group, effect bounds are clamped to the target. An off-screen caster's shadow is then not drawn (K2).
- **Effect-pass GPU time.** It is observed only as the whole frame's `vs-gpu` time. The renderer has no per-pass timestamps.
- **Clip corners in A05.** Pixels within 1.5 px of a clip corner are excluded. P4's corner coverage depends on how the device computes derivatives, and this applies to every clipped draw, not only to effects.
- **"Typical view" in A10.** E26's typical-view rule does not apply to a 50-card scene, so A10 uses the fitted camera, which draws all 50 roots in every frame (U7c amendments).
- **E25 layer bounds inside an effect root.** They are taken at the innermost effect root whether it is drawn or not. This can over-allocate against the E25 layer budget, but never under-allocates (U5c amendments).
- **Instance effects in the panel.** An instance that inherits its source's effects (E41-S10) shows no rows in the Effects section.
- **Export.** PNG and SVG export do not yet draw effects. Section 5.7 gives the SVG mapping. This is the export work's follow-up (V-30).
- **E26 smoke flags.** The E26 smoke project (SwiftShader, not evidence) now uses the Vulkan SwiftShader flags. With `--use-webgpu-adapter` alone, the device was lost under the effect passes.
