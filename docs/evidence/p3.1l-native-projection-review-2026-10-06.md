## Native observations

The two browsers produced identical readbacks for all 158 rows, so they have the same classification:

- 157 rows were CERTIFIED.
- One was TOPOLOGY_REJECTED: `thin/collapse`, reason `topology:orientation:0`, with position pass and squared error 377/2^44.
- No row was POSITION_LIMIT or NUMERIC_UNRESOLVED.

The worst squared displacement was `rectangle/4096x4096/R45/stress`. Its exact value is 118324428103623209/73786976294838206464 ≈ 0.0016036 squared physical pixels. The 1/256 ≈ 0.0039063 bound is unchanged.

Comparison with K's declared JavaScript graph, observation only:

- 83 native clip words differ from K's binary32 NDC words. That is permitted by WGSL evaluation rules and the cause was not observed.
- 24 rows differ in exact `maxSquared` while keeping K's status:
  - 8 carrier S6 rows and 8 carrier S8 rows;
  - 4 rectangle stress and 2 rectangle ordinary rows;
  - 2 origin-sequence rows.
- Of those 24, 22 increased and 2 decreased.
- K's own worst row (≈0.0022892) is the same rectangle stress row, which was smaller natively.

These per-row results are certified only for this browser and adapter. They are not a universal precision theorem and not P3 product acceptance.
