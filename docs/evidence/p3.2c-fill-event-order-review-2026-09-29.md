## Exactness review

Finite Float64 coordinates decode to signed integers in units 2^-1074. The private fixed arrays cover coordinates/directions (33 limbs), line constants/weights (66), homogeneous numerators (99) and comparison differences (165). The largest semantic difference is <2^10497 versus 10560 available bits. Schoolbook products plus an existing word and carry fit u128; occupied-word and terminal-carry assertions prevent truncation. Signed zero normalizes, subtraction trims used length, and widening zero-fills.

Homogeneous line cross products and positive denominator normalization preserve endpoint/segment permutations. Cross multiplication orders rational x and then y without division. The independent generator uses the different parametric intersection formula and exact BigInt rational comparison; it also verifies constructive cases against separately specified analytic expressions. No Number division, production output or fixed-limb helper supplies expected order.

The near-third control is particularly important: 1/(3+2^-52) is strictly less than 1/3, yet both x/y coordinates lie in the same nearest-Float64 rounding cell. Exact midpoint comparisons prove that fact, and representative-only equality is rejected. Four different lines' six pair intersections all compare equal at (1/3,1/3). Successful symbolic ordering does not prove those coordinates can be emitted with a valid placement/topology certificate.
