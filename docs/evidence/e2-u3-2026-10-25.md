# E2-U3 evidence: family-B rung on merged stroke contours, `stroke-v0+r7`, 2026-10-25

Rendering-track item E2, unit E2-U3 of the [E2 contract](../plans/d9-e2-stroke-fast-rung-contract.md#9-units) (E2-K3, `STROKE_B_MAX_POINTS`, the separate rung instance, revision `r7`, E2-A5, E2-D4). Full tier. This is functional evidence. It makes no performance claim (E2-G3), and precision stays **UNVERIFIED** until E3 measures it.

**Result in one line:** E2-K3 is in place and loses no `ok` result, but on the E11-U2 scene it publishes no stroke. Its scene refusals are non-simple outlines (`MONOTONE` 366, every one self-crossing, and `CROSSING` 31), short inner-ring edges (`CLEARANCE` 115) and more than 4,096 vertices (`PREFIX` 84), so the scene stroke count stays at 254 (section "Why the scene gains nothing").

## What changed

- `fill_ladder.rs`: rung 0 takes its caps at run time.
  - `RungCaps { points, edges, contours, sources }` replaces the compile-time sizes. The fill instance uses `FILL_CAPS` (8,192 points and edges, 4 contours, 64 sources, the E14-U1 values), so its stage order, outcomes and bytes are unchanged (E2-R4). `reserve()` is `reserve_with(FILL_CAPS)`.
  - The per-contour and per-source arrays are heap `Vec`s sized by the caps. The FR7 diagonal and half-edge scratch and the output triangle count come from the caps, not constants.
  - `load_rings` is the line-only entry of contract section 6: each ring is one contour, edge `i < n − 1` is owned by a `Line` source and the last edge by the implicit closure. S1 does not run. FR2 runs, unlike contract section 6's wording, but cannot refuse: on segments its control check is exactly zero and AL-FR0 on `e − 2` keeps `δ²` normal, so it only sets each edge's controls (dated note in section 6).
  - FR1's source lookup is indexed on that entry (source `k` is verb `k`, and `last_source` gives each contour's last source), so it is linear in edges rather than quadratic.
  - `run_rings` runs FR1 to FR8 and AL-FR9, and returns `STRUCTURE` if the load was not a ring load.
  - Test-only fault `LadderFault::ClearanceSkipped` (E2-D4) skips FR5.
- `stroke_outline.rs`:
  - `STROKE_B_MAX_POINTS = 4,096` and the stroke instance `STROKE_RUNG_CAPS` (4,096 points and edges, 512 contours, 4,096 sources), reserved once in the stroke workspace inside `STROKE_V0_HEAP_CAP`.
  - After `τ` is known, `build` runs E2-K3: AL-FR0 on `e − 2`, then `PREFIX` above `STROKE_B_MAX_POINTS`, then rung 0 with `δ = τ` under nonzero. A proved path is published with rung 0's mesh: its vertices are the merged contour points, unrounded. Any refusal goes to family A.
  - The arrangement's `InputLimit` (more than 1,024 merged vertices) moved from the front end to `build`, after E2-K3, so that E2-K3 can admit up to 4,096.
  - Test-only switch `no_family_b` is the `r6` path. `no_band_rule` (`r5`) and `unmerged` (`r4`) now also turn E2-K3 off.
- Storage (x86_64, test `e2_u3` storage line): the stroke workspace totals 24,273,908 B of the 48 MiB `STROKE_V0_HEAP_CAP`; the stroke rung is 3,364,508 B of the front end's 7,357,428 B.
- Identity: `STROKE_V0_REVISION` is 7, and the operation id is `geometry-wasm/stroke-v0+r7` in the adapter, its tests, the four GPU specs and the playground comment. B2 §7.1 and the E11 identity row gain dated notes. No later stroke revision is planned in E2.
- Tests (`stroke_merge_tests.rs`): the fixture table gains the `r7` column and is checked on all four revisions; when E2-K3 refuses, `r7` must equal `r6`. New: `e2_a5_family_b_meshes_cover_the_contour_region`, `e2_d4_skipped_clearance_is_caught`, the ignored `e2_u3_differential` (`r4` against `r7` in one run) and E2-K3 outcome tallies per family.

## Why the scene gains nothing

A one-off probe ([`monotone_probe.rs.txt`](e2-u3-2026-10-25/monotone_probe.rs.txt), binary64, with its output) took the 366 scene strokes that E2-K3 refuses with `MONOTONE` and checked their merged contours in binary64:

- 6,588 of 334,610 consecutive edge pairs fail FR4's test (both chords positive along `w = p_end(f) − p_start(e)`); only 42 strokes have at most two such pairs. Half the failing pairs (3,152) turn by 150° to 180° between edges of similar length (hairpins), and 152 reverse exactly; most of the rest turn by 90° to 150°.
- **All 366 contour sets self-cross** (a proper crossing between two non-adjacent edges). So relaxing FR4 for segments would move these strokes to FR5's `CROSSING`, not publish them.

This is the case contract section 6 names under "What falls back": the inner side of joins, round joins and caps, cusp disks and folded offsets make the merged outline non-simple. The scene's strokes are noisy 32-cubic rings, which have them everywhere; section 6 expected E2-K3 to admit closed smooth rings and butt-capped open smooth subpaths. `CLEARANCE` (115) is the short inner-ring edge case of E2-F4, and `PREFIX` (84) is above 4,096 vertices.
