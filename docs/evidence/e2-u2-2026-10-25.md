## E2-P1: the band leaf rule bounds the region distance

The claim: let a leaf be accepted by E2-K2 and the merge use its quad. Then the two-sided Hausdorff distance between the ideal band of the leaf and the merged piece is at most `2^e`. That is the curve share of B2 §3 for a sweep piece, in the metric of the B2 [§3 S1 amendment](../plans/p3-b2-stroke-contract.md#3-precision).

**Notation.** `c(t)`, `t ∈ [a, b]`, is the exact subcurve of the source cubic. `q` are its computed binary64 controls, within `eps = 4·l·u·M` per coordinate of the exact ones (ST-U4, cited). `D_i = 3(q_{i+1} − q_i)` and `V = q_3 − q_0`. `N` is the unit left normal of `c'`. For `|ρ| ≤ r` the parallel curve is `γ_ρ = c + ρ·N`. The ideal band is `P = ∪_ρ γ_ρ`. Its end segments are `c(a) ± r·N(a)` and `c(b) ± r·N(b)`.

| Stage | Statement | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Bounds from the controls | (a) `|c'| ≥ aLow / |V|`: ST-U4's lower bound on `V·c'` over the full cone, with its perturbation term, cited. (b) `c''` is a convex combination of `2(D1 − D0)` and `2(D2 − D1)` of the exact controls. A computed `D_i − D_{i−1}` is within 12·eps per coordinate, so `|c''| ≤ 2·max|ΔD| + 34·eps ≤ c2Up`. (c) `κ ≤ |c''| / |c'|² ≤ c2Up · vUp² / aLow² = Kup`, with `vUp ≥ |V|`. (d) Arc length ≤ the exact control-polygon length, and each `|D_i|` is within 6√2·eps < 9·eps, so the length is at most `lUp`. | `measure_band` |
| 2. Parallel curves | `γ_ρ' = |c'| (1 − ρκ) T`. With `r·Kup ≤ 1/2`, `1 − ρκ ≥ 1/2`, so every `γ_ρ` is regular and its tangent is parallel to `c'`. Its curvature is `κ/(1 − ρκ)`, at most `KO = Kup/(1 − r·Kup)`. Its length is at most `ℓ(1 + r·Kup) ≤ lO`. Its total turning equals `c`'s, at most `KO·lO ≤ π/2`. | fold and turn tests |
| 3. Chord lemma | Take a regular C² curve of length `L` with `|κ| ≤ K` and total turning at most `π/2`, and let `h(σ)` be its signed distance to the chord line, by arc length. Then `h(0) = h(L) = 0` and `|h''| = |cos φ · φ'| ≤ K`, where `φ` is the tangent's angle to the chord. `h − Kσ(L − σ)/2` is convex and zero at both ends, so `h ≤ Kσ(L − σ)/2 ≤ K·L²/8`, and `−h` likewise. The chord's direction is the mean tangent, so it lies inside the tangent angles' interval. That interval is narrower than `π/2`, so `|φ| < π/2` and the projection onto the chord increases strictly from one end to the other. Every curve point therefore lies within `K·L²/8` of the chord segment, and every segment point is the projection of a curve point at that distance. | `chord = KO·lO²/8` |
| 4a. Band into quad | Each `γ_ρ` is within `d = KO·lO²/8` of its chord. That chord is the line `λ = (ρ + r)/2r` of the bilinear patch of the ideal corners `E∓(a), E∓(b)`. Moving the corners to their computed positions, each within `g = gCtrl + gOff` (ST-U4 and ST-G1, as in §4.5), moves each patch point by at most `g`, because the patch is affine in its corners. The computed patch lies inside the computed quad, since the quad is convex and each patch point is a convex combination of its corners. So `P ⊂ quad ⊕ (d + g)`. | `is_band_quad` |
| 4b. Quad into band | A strictly convex quad is the bijective image of its bilinear patch. Each `x` in the quad is `B(λ, μ)`. The ideal `B*(λ, μ)` is within `g` of `x` and lies on the chord of `γ_ρ`. By stage 3 it is within `d` of `γ_ρ ⊂ P`. A kept sample `C` adds the triangle `E−, C, E+` at its seam. That triangle is within `g` of the ideal end segment, which lies in `P`. So `quad ⊕ C ⊂ P ⊕ (d + g)`. | E2-K1 step 1 |
| 5. Acceptance | `d + gCtrl + gOff + kappa·(|V| + 2r) ≤ 2^e(1 − 2^-40)`, where `kappa = 0` because only full-cone leaves are tested. The Hausdorff distance is therefore at most `2^e`. A union of pieces is within the maximum of its pieces' distances. Neighbouring leaves share their computed triples bit for bit, so no seam opens, and E2-K1 is unchanged. | `measure_band` |

Stage 3 replaces the Schur citation of the contract with a direct argument. It gives the same `K·L²/8`. The boundary-to-region step (contract E2-R1) is stages 4a and 4b. It uses the strict convexity of the computed quad, which E2-K2 requires, and no fold, which the fold test gives. The independent review checks this table before merge.

## Departure: ST-O1 gains a band certificate (decided 2026-10-25, 추천안 자동 채택)

ST-O1's cubic-leaf check was B2 §4.5's certificate. It charges the hull term `r·sin θ` between the end normal samples, then checks each side against its chord at matched parameters within the rest of the share. That term is the width-linear term E2-K2 removes, so ST-O1 rejected the `r6` output of E2-F4f, E2-F7, E2-F7r and E2-F8 by construction. A numeric probe ([`hausdorff_probe.rs.txt`](e2-u2-2026-10-25/hausdorff_probe.rs.txt)) samples 401 parameters per leaf in binary64. The share is `2^-3 = 0.125`. Under the standing instruction of 2026-10-24 the recommended option was adopted: a second certificate in ST-O1, derived from the source alone (`certifyBandLeaf`). The alternative was dropping E2-U2 under E2-R1.

- A leaf that section 4.5's certificate rejects is accepted when all of these hold. `C'` has no zero on the closed leaf interval. The band quad of its dumped samples is strictly convex and positive (exact). On rational interval cells of `[a, b]` (the same cell and depth limits as the s1 verifier), three things are certified:
  1. No fold: `r·|C' × C''| < |C'|³`.
  2. Monotone projection: `C'` has a positive component along each side's chord.
  3. The side's distance to its chord line is within budget. The bound is the cell endpoints' exact interval values plus `|O''|·h²/8`, with the s1 verifier's bound on `|O''|`. The larger end-sample mismatch `|O(a) − E(a)|`, `|O(b) − E(b)|` is added, and the total must be within the curve share.
- Items 1 and 2 make each chord point the projection of a side point, so the bound holds both ways between each side and its chord segment. The region step is E2-P1's stages 4a and 4b. The dumped polygon is still checked to be the hull of the six samples, and the centers are still checked within the share.
- When both certificates fail, the finding lists both, the band one prefixed `E2-K2 band certificate:`.
- Unit tests (`tests/unit/geometry-stroke-region.test.ts`): ST-F15 R4 at depth 2, which only the old certificate rejected, now passes. Its true deviation is about 0.001. One R4 leaf at `e = −7` deviates by about 0.0156 and is rejected by both certificates. The positive controls (the s2 chord-normal R4 leaf, the flipped R3 normal) are still rejected.

## Seeded defects (E2-A6) and the hand-built tests

- **E2-D2**, the fold test removed. Caught on E2-F4f (above). As a predicate (`e2_d2_fold_predicate`), a gentle arc is refused in production at `r·Kup ≈ 0.53` and `≈ 1.33`, and accepted without the test. Above 1, `1 − r·Kup < 0` makes the chord bound negative.
- **E2-D3**, the chord bound halved. It does not change E2-F3's leaf set. Its depth-5 leaves have a bound margin below 2, so no depth-4 leaf passes even at half the bound. This is recorded, like ST-D8's named case. E2-F8 shows the defect: 60 leaves instead of 88, which ST-O1 rejects (`stroke-oracle.test.ts`, "E2-U2: the fold control E2-F4f and the seeded defects E2-D2 and E2-D3"). As a predicate (`e2_d3_halved_chord_predicate`), the arc at scale 12 is refused at the full bound and accepted at half.
- **The full cone only** (`e2_k2_needs_the_full_cone_set`). A stationary start, a stationary end and a split range are each refused.
- **S1, from E2-U1** (`e2_s1_sample_outside_seam_and_side_edge_is_kept`). A hand-built band pair has a sample `C = (11, −2)` strictly outside its seam and outside a side edge, and strictly inside the neighbour's quad. `C` stays a vertex, and its polygon is the hull of the quad and `C`. The neighbour does not list it.
- **`-0.0` corner, from E2-U1** (`e2_negative_zero_band_corner_cancels_against_a_hull_neighbour`). A band piece whose start triple carries `-0.0` sits next to a square-cap hull with `+0.0`. Two edge pairs cancel, leaving one contour of twice-area 44, with no `-0.0` vertex.
- **ST-D6** now runs on ST-F15 R4 at `e = −5`. At `r6` R3 there is one leaf, so it has no generated knot to join, while R4 has two. ST-O2 still rejects the defect.

**Re-baselined rows** (work-limit to `ok`, allowed by E2-G1): the ST-F15 listed work-limit cases go from 15 to 12. The native tests that pin B2 §4.5 leaf counts (ST-U4's knot tiling on R3, the controls of ST-D6 to ST-D8) run on `no_band_rule`.
