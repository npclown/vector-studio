### Regressions against rows K4 admits (γ = 4)

| Candidate  | Lost rows                                              | Gained rows               |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |

| K1 @1024   | 24 C, 72 O acceptance, 8 O DPR 3                       | —                         |
| K2c @4096  | 36 C, 108 O, 32 O DPR 3                                | —                         |
| K2c @65536 | 54 C, 149 O, 42 O DPR 3, 20 S (162 of them lane-range) | —                         |

**Why K1 @1024 regresses.** The mechanism is mainly δ̄ growth with T, and only partly slivers.

- **64 of 80 O rows: δ̄ growth.** These rows have no Steiner points. The cell-centre anchor makes |u| and |B| about half a cell in px, so the Shader term grows with T. In the failing rows δ̄ is 1.9 to 4.8 times K4's, and the triangle term then fails.
- **16 O rows: a true Steiner sliver.** These Z/depth-positive rows have a point 0.0062 local units from a vertex on `y = 0`. It also appears at T 256 but passes there, because δ̄ is smaller.

**Where K5 admits but K1 @256 fails.** These are C rows where K4 already fails on the edge term, a near-contact that tiling turns into a vertex term. They are not regressions. K5 passes them only because its assumed δ̄ is about 14 times smaller.

**K2c @65536 lane-range.** The cell-centre anchor exceeds 2^20; for example, `S/TRI/k10/z1/64/d1/I` has `B_x = −2,056,192`.

**Other checks.** Inversions occur only on rows that are already non-admitted (thin/THIN/FTZ). No submesh violation occurs.

## Accepted deviations and notes

**Extra keys.**

- Row keys: `c2Skipped`, `flags`, `rnOutcome`, `uTermMax`, `submesh.reason` and `steiner.packLocus`.
- Trajectory keys: `frames` and `capFrames`.

**Loci.**

- K4 and K5 use the C2 locus `:mesh`.
- Steiner locus strings use tile-local vertex indices. Outcomes are unaffected.

**Sol's triangulator and tile choices** (stable-source rulings): bigint `i` and `j`; cell-centre exactness mapped to lane-range; the compacted index layout; Steiner kind tie-breaks; the stricter ear-rule reading; and seam conformance only where the other side covers.

**Summary and metric definitions.**

- `mMax` is in deterministic insertion order. It is null when no k is admitted.
- K2c's feature `maxCount` is taken over in-window triangles. The feature storage bound reaches 8.0e4 px, because far boundary endpoints are included.

**Implementation notes.**

- The K5 `A` check uses absSum only, which changes no result.
- `triangleMeetsBox` is conservative on degenerate triangles.
- No K4 zero-cost records are emitted.
- The 62 K5 NO_IN_WINDOW_TRIANGLES rows have `inversions: 0` rather than null.
- `sources` omits some unpinned dependencies: `exact.ts`, the corpus and fixture modules, and `exterior.ts`.
- K2c is implemented in `tile-certificate.ts` and `report.ts`; there is no `k2c.ts`.

## Limits

- No GPU run: nothing is shown about clipping, rasterization or f32 fragment evaluation.
- No watertightness claim.
- The exterior and complement partition is excluded.
- K5 is an assumed model, not a certificate.
- K2c has no 1/16-px test and does not evaluate its full K2 obligation.
- Every ADMITTED result is a necessary-condition result under these limits.

## Next decision

D1, the mechanism, is a user decision. Observed, without adoption:

- K1 @256 is the only certified candidate that admits all three failing class A rows with no regression against K4 and M_max ≥ 40.
- Its cost is up to 45-66 tiles per frame on 1280 × 720.
- A larger T trades fewer tiles for δ̄ growth and regressions.
