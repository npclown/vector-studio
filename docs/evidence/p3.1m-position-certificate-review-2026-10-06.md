# P3.1m position certificate evidence review, 2026-10-06

This record reviews the N01-N03 implementation of the [frozen position certificate contract](../plans/p3-position-certificate-contract.md). The contract was integrated as `d9fe6f9` through PR #100. Amendments A1 and A2 were added during this checkpoint. The deterministic [report](p3.1m-position-certificate/report.json) has SHA-256 `8bc1eb2d7cb38be6afee114a69817ac22f0d138f9a641024e39489d77b902351`. The test regenerates it and requires it to match byte for byte.

## Scope and limits

This is a test-only, host-only, exact evaluation of a sufficient condition. It is not a runtime check, an adopted representation, an origin rule or rejection behavior. Native words come only from the archived L records: one adapter, fixture origin only. Residual risk R1, rewrites outside family F, remains. User decisions U1-U4 are open. No GPU, browser or benchmark run was made, and none was needed.

## Reviews and amendments

- **Implementation.** Primary implemented N01. An independent Sol medium worker implemented N02/N03. There was no recursive delegation. Primary's scratch run of N01 reproduced every prospective value before N02/N03 integration.
- **Amendment A1.** The literal for the input-magnitude row could not isolate guard check 4, so it was replaced with a 4096x8 row. The expected outcomes did not change. The reviewer judged it a legitimate literal fix, not an output-driven expectation change.
- **Amendment A2.** This documents three implementation clarifications, none of which changes an outcome: the sound shared `Plo` minimum, the overlapping-fan outcome, and the window lane bound.
- **Independent Astra high review.**
  - Phase 1 cleared the stable source. Its only should-fix was A2.
  - Phase 2 recomputed the report independently with BigInt rationals and found 0 mismatches across 158 rows and every sampled value. Verdict: **EVIDENCE ACCEPTED**.
  - NIT 6 was applied: the test now asserts that EXT-32768 is the only Γ9 flip.
  - The remaining NITs were judged harmless: the mutated-summary control runs through the test's equal copy of the clearance logic, and N02 flushes to +0 after rounding.

## Open after this record

The certificate shows that the carrier can be admitted conservatively for ordinary on-screen meshes. It also shows that P1's current origin rule cannot certify an on-screen mesh at zoom 64, DPR 2, whereas a physical-window origin can, and that large offscreen extents and sub-δ features are rejected. Adoption requires the user decisions in the contract:

- **U1:** domain;
- **U2:** failure behavior;
- **U3:** origin;
- **U4:** error-outcome reuse.

C04 items 1 and 3-6, raster coverage, deferred stroke work and full P3 acceptance remain open.

## Erratum (2026-10-06)

The "O-PX at low zoom" bullet above misstated the O-PX effect. The report itself is unchanged and correct.

**Corrected counts:**

- O-PX newly rejects 12 rows that the fixture origin admits:
  - 8 W bowtie and zero-closure rows (S4 and S7), `clearance:triangle`;
  - 4 Y-S5 rows at zoom 1 and DPR 1.5, `clearance:fan`.
- 8 Y S4/S7 rows that were already rejected change their reason from `fan` to `triangle`.

**Corrected cause:** the effect is not only a low-zoom effect. g reaches 2^16 at zoom 0.01 and DPR 1, and 2^15 at DPR 2.

The independent reviewer of the U2 proposal found this.
