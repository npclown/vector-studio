## Where the v2 time goes

The records time `encodeAndSubmitMs` (scene encode and GPU submit, on the main thread) and the frame interval; nothing else in the frame is timed. The rest of the main thread's work per frame is the difference, which this sitting does not split further.

| Scenario | `encodeAndSubmitMs` median, v1 → v2 | Frame median, v1 → v2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| typical view, edit drag | 6.6 to 8.5 → 9.7 to 11.0 | 16.7 → 33.3 |
| all-edit (camera F) | 44.0 to 49.3 → 52.3 to 56.6 | 50 to 66.6 → 66.7 to 66.8 |
| all-pan (camera F) | 57.3 to 73.2 → 67.8 to 82.2 | 66.6 to 83.2 → 83.2 to 83.5 |
| stress, pan and zoom | 9.5 to 10.7 → 9.4 to 10.6 | 16.7 → 66.6 to 66.7 |

- **Stress v2: the column, not the renderer.** The scene and its encode are v1's (about 10 ms), and the frame goes from 16.7 to 66.6 ms. The only change is `ui=1` with the layer tree at 10,000 rows. The pan and zoom path changes no document, so the cost is the column's work or its layout on each camera step; which of the two is not measured. `LayerTree` (`packages/ui/src/layers.tsx`) renders every row of `layerRows` with no virtualization.
- **Typical view v2: mostly outside encode.** Encode is about 2 to 3 ms above v1 (the instances and text), and the frame doubles to 33.3 ms. The edit drag changes the document on every step, so the column (655 layer rows and the property panel showing the selection) re-renders each frame, as the contract's "panel cost" risk expected.
- **All-visible v2: the renderer first, the column on top.** Encode alone is 52 to 82 ms at F, above the 33.3 ms ceiling before the column adds anything. This is E30's CPU-bound miss from E26, plus about 8 to 10 ms of v2 content.
- **Pointer latency v2.** Per-run medians are 222 to 592 ms busy and 195 to 305 ms plain, against 61 to 86 ms in v1. Each move changes the document, so the column re-renders per move, and the moves queue behind it.

## Long tasks, font load, autosave and typing

- **Long tasks outside isolation** (over 50 ms):
  - v1 busy: 12 of 20 runs have one (57 to 78 ms); v1 plain: 2 of 20 (59 and 69 ms).
  - v2 busy: every run has 4 to 6, the worst 198 to 254 ms per run; v2 plain: 4 of 20 runs have one (55 to 62 ms).
- **The font-load change (v2 busy).** It loaded in all 20 runs. The text port's generation advanced three times (3 to 6) in every run, and the `m9-font-load` measure took 1.03 to 1.92 s. Four or five long tasks over 50 ms overlap it in each run, most of them 170 to 250 ms: the re-projection of every text unit on page 1, as the contract's "text under load" risk expected. They are counted outside isolation and are not exempt (M9-Q3).
- **Isolation measure (busy).** v1 120 to 165 ms; v2 136 to 173 ms.
- **Autosave.** No write starts while the pointer is pressed, in any of the 80 latency runs. The held write starts after the pointer-up handling at 61.0 to 81.2 ms (v1 busy) and 74.6 to 97.4 ms (v2 busy), about four to six frames, so "within one frame" is not met as measured, as in U6b. In the plain runs no write is due during the press, and the first write follows the 500 ms debounce (515.8 to 540.3 ms).
- **Typing** (`m9-typing-10k/v1`, recorded only, M9-Q4): 40 keys per run, none without a change. Per-run medians of `keyToWorkDoneMs` 40.2 to 58 ms, p95 65.5 to 88.3 ms, maximum 80.3 to 150.4 ms. Each run has two or three long tasks over 50 ms (53 to 61 ms).
- **UI geometry** (`m9-ui-geometry`, M9-A03): the canvas origin (1, 74) and size (1280 × 720) are equal with and without `ui=1` in all four projects.

## For gate G1

- Section 2: the platform and the 10,000-node document pass; the worst case, typical editing, stress and pointer priority fail on the judged workload (v2, `ui=1`, M9-Q1 (a)).
- Two kinds of cost were found:
  - **The right column** (layer tree and property panel). It alone turns a passing stress scene (16.8 ms) into 83 ms, doubles the typical-view frame, and is the likely main part of the v2 latency. This is the "panel cost" item that G1 (a) files as new backlog and G1 (b) names as a lever.
  - **Text re-projection on a font load**: three long tasks of about 200 ms each.
- The renderer's own CPU cost at camera F (E30) still exceeds 33.3 ms before the column is counted.
- No v1 row regressed against U6b once the spread between sittings is taken into account.
