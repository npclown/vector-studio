## Disclosures

1. **A10's cost.** The frame interval equals the idle one, and every drag move was applied, so the scene keeps pace on this PC. But the GPU latency proxy (`vs-gpu`, submit to `onSubmittedWorkDone`) is above one frame interval.
   - It rose about 7 ms over plain `e41-cards`, which measured 10.6 to 12.9 ms in E41's sitting. V-35's 15 blend roots measured 7 to 8 ms.
   - The steady pacing bounds the GPU's busy time per frame at about 17.7 ms; how much of the 18 to 20 ms proxy is busy time, and how much is callback delay and contention, is unmeasured (no timestamp queries). So ten radius-20 background blurs use at most, and possibly close to, the whole frame on a GTX 1660 SUPER. More glass, or a display that really paces at 60 Hz, could drop frames.
   - Two more departures in reading the row:
     - "Typical view" is read as E26's `fitted` camera.
     - The drag moves `card-22`, which is not a glass card.
   - Glass cards 0, 10, and so on keep their `e41-cards` layer blur.
   - If the user accepts, the recommendation is a backlog item to reduce the cost: blur the backdrop at reduced resolution for large radii, and share snapshots between neighbouring roots.
2. **A09's add steps** are checked through the panel and the model state. Pixel probes start at the edit step, because the default inner shadow and a radius-4 background blur change too little to probe robustly.
3. **Contract section 12, carried from U3:**
   - At antialiased clip edges, the blend-root merge is not clipped.
   - A parent that dissolves into its group counts as nested.
   - The panel's nested rule counts container blend modes other than `passThrough`, and `normal` among them.
   - Disclosure (4) of that section, no fixture combining blend and background blur, is closed by G10 and G10b.
4. **Nested background blur.** A background blur draws only on roots whose parent is the main target. Nested ones are skipped, counted in `backgroundBlurSkipped`, and explained in the panel. `backgroundBlurSkipped` is a counter beside `effectPasses`; a host-visible statistic is backlog.
5. **Export.** PNG and SVG export of effects is still not implemented. This is V-30's follow-up and outside V-36.
6. **Superseded and untested cases.** The pre-U3 renderer fallback (a background blur on `'v1'` before U3) was tested in U2 and replaced by U3's nested and missing-pipeline paths. Release of `D` and `B` on device loss and dispose is not tested with them in flight ([u3-swiftshader.md](u3-swiftshader.md)).
7. **Provenance.** The GPU-spec records carry no browser, version or commit field; the browser versions and `366d34e` are as relayed by the coordinator. The Chrome and Edge records are byte-identical (plausible on one GPU and driver, as for E41's PC records).
