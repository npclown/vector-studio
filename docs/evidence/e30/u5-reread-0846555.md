## Conditional levers (E30-A07)

- **L3, backend encode or GPU: called for.** After L1 and L2, `vs-submit` p95 is 5.2 to 5.7 ms in the typical view and 39 to 51 ms in the all-visible rows, and `vs-gpu` p95 is 47 to 329 ms in every row but stress: both over section 4.3's 5 ms. Prepare is now the smaller CPU part everywhere except pan; submit (the backend's encode, unchanged by L1 and L2) and the queue are what remain. U5 profiles the backend on the PC and takes the leading cost; a remedy that needs a new pipeline, render bundle or a draw-order change is written up as its own item first.
- **L5, the renderer's share of latency: not needed.** The renderer's part of a median move is about 7 ms (prepare 2.7 to 3.0, submit 4.2), under the 10 ms threshold. The scene set apply runs inside the handler (10.9 ms median) and is not measured on its own on the PC; a container observation at `0846555` (not evidence) puts a one-node set's `applyChanges` on a 10,000-node mirror at 0.04 ms in the median (0.16 ms p95, 300 sets), with the next view build at 0.006 ms, so the apply adds well under 1 ms. The handler's remaining share is outside the renderer (plan section 5).
- **L4** (taken, #489): the plain rows' post-isolation long tasks are gone.
