## Findings

- **E26-A06 passes in all 20 runs, with wide margins.**
  - One rejection takes 95 to 118 ms, and 16 take 121 to 146 ms. Both are well under the bounds, which come to about 300 ms and 600 ms at R ≈ 83 to 96 ms.
  - Every isolation refused 15 sets for one target and 181 for 16. Its `editor.scene-rejected` IDs equalled the targets, so there was no resync fallback and no wider exclusion.
  - The discarded warm-up isolation took 127 to 141 ms.
  - The tail of a clean load's command, outside R, is 79 to 97 ms. The isolation measure holds this tail as well, which A06 reads as "rejection to end of command".
  - Injected rejections refuse at once, so these times are a lower bound for a real mirror rejection.
- **E26-A03's stress row passes again** in every project, at 16.8 ms.
- **The autosave hold (U6a) works.** Across the 40 busy and plain runs, no autosave write starts during the press.
  - In the busy runs, the held write starts 61.6 to 76.0 ms after the pointer up was handled.
  - In the plain runs, no write is due during the press, so the first write follows the commit's 500 ms debounce (517 to 531 ms).
  - A05 also asks for the held write to start "within one frame after pointer up". As measured, that is not met: from the pointer-up handling, 62 to 76 ms is about four 16.7 ms frames. The records carry no frame timestamps around the release, so a frame-relative check was not possible. While the press runs, the main thread is behind queued moves: move medians are 62 to 90 ms. The release's frame and task come after that work.
- **E26-A05 still misses on long tasks.** 14 of the 20 busy runs have one or two long tasks of 55 to 82 ms outside isolation; in the re-run it was 11 of 20, at 57 to 77 ms. Most start right after the isolation task, during the press. Two busy runs (chrome-dpr1 repetition 5, chrome-dpr2 repetition 1) also have one of 68 and 70 ms at the pointer up, and one plain run (edge-dpr1, repetition 4) has one of 72 ms there.
- **E26-A04 still misses:**
  - busy: 151 to 327 ms;
  - plain: 136 to 237 ms;
  - medians: 62 to 90 ms.
- **E26-A03's all-visible rows still miss at 66.8 to 100 ms.** Their `encodeAndSubmitMs` p95 is 56 to 89 ms, so they remain CPU-bound.
- **E26-A02 regressed in Chrome against the re-run, and the cause is not established.**
  - Every run's median is still 16.7 ms.
  - Five Chrome runs have a p95 of 33.3 to 33.4 ms: one at DPR 1 and four at DPR 2. Their frame counts are 515 to 564 in the 10 s window, against about 580 elsewhere.
  - 11 of the 20 view-edit runs have one or two long tasks of 50 to 59 ms (10 counting only those over 50 ms, as `summary.json`'s `longTasksOver50` does). The re-run had none.
  - Edge holds 16.8 ms, as before.
  - Since `59bf080` the code paths this scenario runs gained two things: U6a's press callback in `CanvasInput` and `EditorCanvas`, which does no autosave work without `?persist=1`, and the bench's `isolation=` flag, which leaves the service unwrapped in frame scenarios. The other processes running on the PC are the other candidate. E30 owns finding out.

- **E26-A08 on the reference PC.** The full `pnpm test:gpu` ran at `8629d30` after a rebuild (geometry 1,217 passed, root `pnpm build`), from 2026-11-01 11:05 to 15:31 KST (4.4 h), on Chrome and Edge.
  - 209 of 210 tests passed, none skipped or flaky, and no committed capture or golden file changed.
  - One failed: `[chrome] viz-v3-integration.spec.ts` "V-3 A07 integration page at DPR 2" hung until the 30-minute test timeout, then reported `page.evaluate: Target page, context or browser has been closed` in its `snapshot()` hook. The same test passed on Chrome DPR 1 and on Edge at both DPRs.
  - Run alone once afterwards, with the same commit and build, it passed in 49.7 s.
  - The hang fell inside a window (12:13 to 12:43 KST) when the remote session to the PC was offline, so a window or session-state effect cannot be ruled out. The suite's `line` reporter prints no per-test times, so the test's time in the other projects is not known.
