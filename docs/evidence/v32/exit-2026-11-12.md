## Disclosures

- Section 10 of the contract still holds. In particular:
  - Images are at most 4096 per side on the GPU.
  - The texture budget is 256 MiB.
  - Mips are built on the main thread.
  - Mirroring does not flip images.
  - Animated images draw their first frame.
  - There is no colour management.
- The node-environment unit suite has one failure, `p3-b0r-identity`. It fails the same way on `main` in the Node 22 container and is not touched by V-32.
- Open follow-ups from the U4 review r3, none blocking:
  - An orientation error in a header for an image over 4096 per side would draw distorted, because the size check cannot see it. The parser matches Chromium on 96 probed files.
  - The Fill panel thumbnail decodes the whole image.
  - Choosing "Image" in the Fill type select relies on the transient user activation of the pick.
- The first A06 and A09 headed runs (`41dbc9dc`, `6f4b2c6c`) predate V-35's blend renderer (#588, `6f94aa2e`), which changed `native-primitive-scene.ts` and the E23 check page. The recheck on `main` `6e6eb1f8`, after #588, passed 17/17.
- `tests/e41-next-host/e41-effects.spec.ts` fails on `main` because of V-36's refusal text change, not V-32's. The V-36 thread was told.
