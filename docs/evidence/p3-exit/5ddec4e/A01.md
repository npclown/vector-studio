## Notes

- The sweep's conformity check finds a vertex strictly inside a boundary edge of the stored mesh. Interior T-junctions, which the kernel's meshes may have (E3-K2), are counted by the A04 harness and measured by the readback. Under the f32 mapping they open the cracks of the last row.
- The measured cracks are about a thousandth of a device pixel. A crack drops a pixel only when a pixel centre falls inside it. V-4's A08 run reports such pixels as A01 evidence when it finds them ([V-4 contract](../../../plans/viz-v4-a08-corpus-contract.md), "Cracks"); none is cited here.
- Other adapters and browsers stay UNVERIFIED.
