# MVP exit record (2026-11-02)

**Status: PROPOSED.** The user accepts or declines this record on a decision card (M9-A08). Until then the MVP exit is not made. This record is unit M9-U5 of the [M9 contract](../plans/mvp-m9-performance-exit-contract.md#7-mvp-exit-record-m9-u5). Its form is set by contract section 7, and the definition of "MVP done" by the [MVP editor plan, section 1](../plans/mvp-editor-plan.md#1-goal-and-definition-of-mvp).

It lists, for every bullet of [`requirements.md`](../requirements.md), the evidence and one verdict. It also lists the coverage rows with their milestone acceptances, the milestones and what each carries, the rendering defects carried from P3 and P4, the performance verdicts and the G1 decision, the known limits, the backlog and the owed user actions. It makes no claim beyond the records it cites. Every PR is cited by number; every machine is named by role only (the main PC is the reference machine, the laptop has an Intel iGPU and is not evidence).

- **Three requirement bullets are UNVERIFIED** (R33 real Korean IME, R34 system fonts by name, R68 the path-rendering "not failures" cases; sections 1.5 and 1.10).

## Verdict words

- **PASS**: the cited criterion passed and its milestone exit was accepted by the user.
- **PASS with disclosure**: as PASS, with a limit the cited record discloses; the limit is named in the row.
- **FAIL**: a measured miss.
- **UNVERIFIED**: no cited record shows the behavior; the reason is in the row.
- **Deferred**: deferred by the requirements text itself.

Precision of every renderer output stays UNVERIFIED against the 0.25 px target, as every milestone record says, because P3 exited with A04 UNVERIFIED (section 4.1). Rows about drawn output carry this disclosure without repeating it. Headed evidence of every milestone M1 to M9 is from the main PC only, in Chrome and Edge 156, with Playwright input; the P3 exit evidence used Chrome 155 and Edge 156 ([E11 section 13](../plans/d9-e11-p3-c03-c05-closure-contract.md#13-p3-exit-decision-2026-10-27)).
