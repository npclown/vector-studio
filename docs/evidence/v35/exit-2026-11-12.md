## Disclosures

1. **A10 and the display.** The ceiling assumes a 60 Hz display. On this sitting the idle frame interval was already 17.5 to 18.0 ms. Windows reports 60 Hz, the only mode at 1920 × 1080, and the coordinator found no setting that differs. Earlier E41 sittings on this PC measured 16.7. The scene holds one vsync with the GPU at about half of it. As in the E26 U5d record, this is a miss under the no-rounding rule, not a cost found in the scene. A10 also names 1920 × 1080 at DPR 1; the E26 harness measures 1280 × 720 CSS px at DPR 1 and DPR 2, read at DPR 2 as for E41.
2. **A05's amended comparison.** The tolerance stays 2/255 for the modes whose slope is at most 1. Steeper modes get their slope times one backdrop step beyond the first (colorBurn about 1.8/255 more, colorDodge about 4). The amendment was made after a reference-PC miss. Its reasoning (stored values one step off: a tie at 229.5 and a non-tie at 175.75) is inferred from matching the GPU's outputs, not from reading the backdrop back. R3/R4 [90, 280] passes with about 0.27/255 of margin.
3. **Panel.** `Pass through` is offered only when every selected node is a container or instance. A mixed selection where each node holds at most one fill edits as one Fill row with mixed values (M4's behaviour, with V-39's binding). Fills that differ, where one node holds two or more, show "Mixed" with a replacing `+`. The M4 and V-39 contracts record this.
4. **Host usage.** `editorRendererOptions` turns on `blend: 'v0'` for every host that uses it, which adds `TEXTURE_BINDING` to the canvas (the E10 section 3.2 amendment). A10's cost includes it.
