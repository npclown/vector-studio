## 3. E14-Q1 class test in the A01 sweep

[E14 section 10](../plans/d9-e14-fill-rung0-edge-cap-contract.md#10-questions-and-decisions), Q1 (a), adds the closed-list class "fill `work-limit` on a self-crossing path over E" and says the A01 sweep applies it "with its own exact predicates on the P2 flattening of the row". It was not yet in the sweep.

- `tests/geometry/p3-exit-sweep/fill-class.ts`: mirrors the P2 flattening (binary subdivision of the cubic relative to its first point until the control points are within the tolerance of the chord's third points, with P2's rounding guard, depth 20, and its per-cubic, visit and output-verb caps, on which it refuses the path as P2 does; `geometry.rs` `visit`). Flatness uses a port of musl's `hypot`, which `f64::hypot` is on `wasm32-unknown-unknown`; it matched a Rust-built WASM `hypot` on 2,000,000 random pairs, which V8's `Math.hypot` does not guarantee. It merges consecutive equal points (`==`, like the kernel's `same_point`) and the closing duplicate, counts flat edges, and finds a proper crossing with an exact `orient2d` (binary64 filter with Shewchuk's error bound, then a BigInt determinant). A row is in the class when its outcome is `work-limit`, it has more than E = 256 edges, and two edges cross properly.
- `run.check.ts` writes `fillRejections` (per rejected fill request: outcome, flat edges, crossing pair, class) and `fillClassCounts` (per stratum and named or generated row, since E11 section 7.2 rules them differently) into each phase record; the tool prints the counts. The sweep's existing checks, the nine controls per kind, the freeze gate and the whole-batch failure rule are unchanged.
- `tests/unit/p3-exit-sweep-fill-class.test.ts`: E14-F5 (scene path 3) is in the class with 3,463 edges, the count the kernel test pins on its own S1 polygon; a simple circle over E is not; a crossing figure-eight is out at E edges and in at E + 2; only `work-limit` is classified; touching and collinear edges are not proper crossings; `orient2d` is exact where binary64 is not, also for subnormal coordinates, where the filter's products underflow and it falls back to the exact path; a path over P2's own caps is refused; `-0` and `+0` merge.
- Reading (this record's own): "S1 polygon" is taken as the P2 flattening with merged repeated points. The kernel's `collect_contours` rejects a repeated cubic leaf (`ZeroLengthLeaf`) instead of merging it; a zero-length edge cannot cross properly, so only the edge count can differ, by the merged points. The kernel's S1 polygon is not read.
- **Open for E11-U3 (E14 section 10 and E14-R3).** Q1 (a) takes crossings that only the flattening makes (a simple curve passing within the tolerance of itself) into the class and says "the A01 record reports them"; E14-R3 asks for their minimum source distance where it can. This test does not tell such members from true self-crossing paths. Every scene member is a looping path; the corpus members are not separated. The A01 record at the snapshot must add that report, or the review must accept its absence. The scene run cross-checks the mirror: E14-F6 (scene path 500, 9,284 flat verbs with MOVE and CLOSE in the [E14 cause record](e14-fill-work-limit-cause-2026-10-24.md)) gets 9,282 edges here.
