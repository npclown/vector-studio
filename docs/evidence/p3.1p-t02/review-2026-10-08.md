### Gate 2 failures

All three class A rows are admitted. The 16 failing variants are genuine grid hazards.

- **`S/TRI/k{20,24,32,40}/z1/d1/{I,R15}/cam:v1` (8).** These are the pre-stated thin-tip ids, but their failing vertex pairs extend beyond v1's incident cells. Examples:
  - k20 I has kind-1 records at `x = 2^20 − 512` and a kind-4 corner two cells from v1;
  - k32 R15 has pairs eight cells away.

  The thin tip spans several cells. The frozen predicate is narrower than the hazard, so these rows correctly do not qualify.
- **`S/RECT/k{20,24,32,40}/z1/d1/{I,R15}/cam:v2` (8).** This hazard was not pre-stated. The 0-2 diagonal passes about `256/16385 ≈ 0.0156` local units from a grid corner (k20 I). That makes a near-coincident vertex pair created by the grid.

### Gate 3 losses: attribution

The reviewer re-ran `c2Merged` on the same merged meshes for all 100 losses, crossing scale (`s_lo` or the row's `s0`), δ (the row's T02 Ē or the archived T01 δ̄), and triangle set (all drawn, or region only).

- At `s0` with T01's δ̄, region only: **all 100 pass**. This holds even over every region triangle in the candidate set, not only T01's in-window ones. A per-tile run passes too.
- At `s0` with T01's δ̄, all drawn triangles: 24 fail. That is the fringe contribution.
- At `s0` with the T02 Ē, region only: 68 fail.
- `Ē_T02 / δ̄_T01` is 1.39 at minimum, 1.75 median and 2.31 at maximum.

**Causes.** The losses come from δ and scale, not from new geometry in the larger window:

1. S3 at `s_lo`, up to 2× below the row's scale. This was a pre-stated risk.
2. The epoch Ē growing with the zoom band (`s_hi` up to about 2× `zq`) and with the record extent of the swept window. This was not pre-stated.
3. Fringe exterior triangles.

Primary's first attribution blamed the last 48 rows on clipped slivers entering the larger window. The reviewer refuted it, and it is withdrawn.

## Accepted deviations and notes

**Boundary pieces.**

- Edges on a candidate-set outer side, whose neighbour is not a candidate, are excluded from boundary pieces unless they lie on an original boundary edge. Boundary vertices on that border get no sector entry.
- This is sound: those edges lie in non-candidate closed squares, which do not meet `P̂`, so they lie more than γ from `V`. This is recorded as the accepted reading of E3's "edges used by exactly one region triangle".

**Sol's builder choices** (accepted in stable-source review):

- region winding from the first non-degenerate source triangle, with S4 catching mixed winding;
- side points;
- kind-4 classification;
- S4 check order: orientation, duplicate record, T-junction, edge use, area;
- O02 routines copied into `fringe.ts`;
- per-cell translation and LCM scaling.

**Build-time failures** are labelled `partition:*` before S1-S3. This fails closed, and none occurs.

**Harness details.**

- Trajectory frames carry no W or H, so the row's W and H are kept.
- The epoch lower bound is open at zoom `2^-20`.
- `c2Failures` holds 0/1 flags per term, not counts.
- `builds`, `cellsPerEpochStart`, `liveCarriers` and `anchorUploads` are upper bounds from box tests.
- The G3 band check runs against the trajectory cell set, which already contains every candidate within `ρ_L`. It therefore does not validate the real fringe set.

## Next decision

T02 failed gates 2 and 3, so the R2 contract needs a user decision before O03. Candidate directions for a revision 4, none adopted:

- **Reduce δ.**
  - Finer zoom sub-bands with recertification and no rebuild.
  - A localized C2 that charges each pair or triangle only its own vertices' E, instead of a global Ē.
- **Improve the fringe triangulation quality.**
- **Grid hazards (gate 2).** Mitigate the thin-tip and near-diagonal grid slivers, for example with a per-mesh grid phase or snapping Steiner points (which revisits D3(a)), or accept them as a class A product boundary.
