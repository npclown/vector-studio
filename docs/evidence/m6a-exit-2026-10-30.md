## Known limits and disclosed findings

- **Browser layout is not ours** (contract section 12): breaks and fallback can change with browser updates. The headed fixtures are structural, with the committed test font, and every headed result is from one machine in Chrome and Edge 156.
- **Scale steps** (section 3.2): a node whose largest size or extent bound crosses a scale step can move by up to `size / 2048` px per metric, so a line break within that residual of the width can flip on an edit. Sizes above 32 get a smaller `s`. A request already past the layout-unit range at `s` 1 is measured unscaled and can clamp as before.
- **Low `lineHeight`** (section 4 disclosure): the zero-size strut makes a line box always reach its baseline, so below a font-dependent value the line pitch is larger than `lineHeight × size`.
- **Alignment with trailing spaces before an LF**: a right- or centre-aligned line draws those spaces past its alignment point, as the fake does (U4 note 2).
- **Font loads briefly blank large text**: each generation bump (two per `registerFont`, plus one per `loadingdone`) makes E28 re-rasterize every visible cluster at up to `E28_RASTER_PER_FRAME`, 64 since E28 #378 (`b0571f7`). At 64, 256 visible clusters take 4 frames and 1,000 take 16. M6b and M9 should know this.
- **Test font approval** (M6a-Q13 (a)): recorded under the user's standing rule of 2026-10-24 (approval-needed decisions take the recommended option, recorded and notified). The user was notified on 2026-10-29 with the asset's name, version, license and hash. The M6a decision card is still open, and a later pick of (b) removes the asset. M6a-A14 reads this as "the user's recorded approval"; the user may judge otherwise.
- **Rendering-track reviews**: U4's rendering-track review is recorded as r2 READY; the r3 delta was sent and no further result is recorded. U4c changed text-dom's measured values (finer positions, same interface) and had one independent review, with no separate rendering-track review recorded. A14 asks for both tracks on U2 and U4 only.
- **No text is drawn and nothing is edited** in M6a: next-host draws no text before M6c, and there is no text tool, edit mode, caret or IME before M6b. A text node projects nothing (A10).
- The M6a exit does not establish M6 or MVP completion; M6b, M6c, M4, M8 and M9 remain.
