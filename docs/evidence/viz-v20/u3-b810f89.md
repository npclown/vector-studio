**What V-20 changed on the v1 path.**

- v1 runs with the bench aside (`AppearancePanel`) and no layer tree.
- L1 memoizes `CanvasSide`, which returns that aside, so the aside no longer re-renders on camera-only changes. L2 and L3 do not reach v1.
- A memo removes renders. It can lower a v1 row: it fits all-edit chrome-dpr1 and edge-dpr2 falling from 83.3 to 66.7 ms. It is not a likely cause of a rise.

**Regression check (the six † rows).**

- **all-pan edge-dpr2: 83.3 against 66.8 ms (+24.7 %).**
  - Three of five runs are at 83.3 ms and two at 66.7 to 66.8 ms. Every run's median is 66.6 ms.
  - `encodeAndSubmitMs` p95 is 65 to 70.2 ms, about 5 ms above M9-U2's 63.8 to 65.2 ms in this project.
  - The frame interval is a whole number of refresh intervals, so a p95 moving from four to five intervals moves it 25 %.
  - M9-U2's all-pan rows were 99.9 and 83.2 ms in the Chrome projects.
- **Pointer v1, busy (three projects) and plain (two): +11.3 to +24.7 %.**
  - Neither number shows more work per move. Per-run medians are 59.2 to 83.7 ms (M9-U2: 61 to 86 ms), and long tasks outside isolation stay at 0 or 1 per run. The rises are in the p95 tail.
  - Each project's spread is wide. Busy edge-dpr1 runs from 157.4 to 334.2 ms over five runs.
  - U6b's own maximum over projects was 326.8 ms busy and 236.6 ms plain.
  - The likely cause is the open apps, the background-load candidate U6b named. It is not proven.
