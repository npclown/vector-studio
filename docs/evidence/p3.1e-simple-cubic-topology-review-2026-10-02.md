## Proof and source review

Exact de Casteljau restriction of the original represented cubic determines each leaf's controls. Actual returned knots must equal its endpoints. Nonnegative derivative-control projections onto a nonzero chord, with at least one positive, prove strict interior projection monotonicity. Linear interpolation from each cubic leaf to its chord remains injective inside its control hull. Disjoint nonadjacent hulls and adjacent hull intersections consisting only of the fixed shared endpoint establish a simultaneous family of embedded contours. Exact implicit closure participates in the same checks. Orientation and component nesting therefore persist; positional closeness alone is never used to prove topology.

Primary and an independent mathematical audit reviewed the proof and all 47 analytic expectations before first fixture execution. The generator uses direct exact Bernstein evaluation, independently from the oracle's rational de Casteljau restrictions. The prior P3.1d generator was only exposed with a default-preserving source-ordinal argument; its corpus and numeric conversion remain unchanged. Closed hull checks cover proper crossings, endpoint/edge contacts, collinear overlaps and containment. Exact shoelace orientation and the existing exact line-fill classifier label the already-separated components.

Review clarified preflight source-line counts, status precedence and copied point ownership before execution. Stable-source review found one implementation defect: an earlier implicit closure could consume the final leaf slot, but the next source projection ran before checking that limit. The owner moved the limit check first. A C04 plus negative-projection regression requires WORK_LIMIT with 13 leaves and zero pairs. Primary and independent re-review confirm the correction. Additional controls pin adjacent overlap, exact pair-stage counters, late input/provenance precedence and independent returned-point ownership.

The frozen corpus retains 40 transformed base cases, five contour-order permutations and two exact-linear extreme squares. Every source cubic also passes the unchanged P3.1d 1/8-pixel positional certificate at identity screen. Tiny loops, negative projection, bowtie crossings, contacting/coincident contours and analytically overlapping near-contact hulls remain distinct rejection controls. A valid contour may be UNRESOLVED when this sufficient proof cannot certify it; that is not a product rejection rule.
